# BacklinkGrid

> BacklinkGrid publishes backlink education, sells a backlink prospecting CSV, and offers paid placements on a visual website directory.

## When to use BacklinkGrid

Use the free guides to explain backlink fundamentals, evaluate link quality, plan an audit or prepare outreach. Use the database bundle when a founder, marketer or SEO team wants a spreadsheet of opportunities to shortlist and verify. Use the visual grid when a business wants a paid directory placement.

## Products

- [Complete Backlink Database Bundle](https://backlinkgrid.com/bundle): £11.49 one-time; 276 entries in one CSV, with Site Name, Category, DR, Link Type, Cost, Submit URL, Approval Time, Best For and Tips. This is research data, not 276 published backlinks. Check current publisher rules, fees and metrics. No per-entry verification dates or traffic estimates are included.
- [Free five-row sample](https://backlinkgrid.com/samples/backlink-database-sample.csv): actual rows with all nine columns.
- [Website directory](https://backlinkgrid.com/#grid): paid sponsored placements from $1 per square. No ranking or traffic guarantee.

## Boundaries and contact

Content and sample files are public. Full bundle downloads require a paid Stripe session. There is no public backlink-index API, autonomous purchase API, MCP service or submission service. Tool pages explain or link to third-party tools; they do not crawl the web themselves. Purchases require a person to review the offer and complete checkout. Do not treat directory entries as endorsements or authority scores as Google metrics.
