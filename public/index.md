# BacklinkGrid: backlink research and website discovery

BacklinkGrid helps founders, marketers and SEO teams learn about backlinks, research opportunities and promote websites through a visual directory.

## Complete Backlink Database Bundle

The [Complete Backlink Database Bundle](https://backlinkgrid.com/bundle) costs £11.49 as a one-time purchase. It contains 276 entries in one CSV with nine columns: Site Name, Category, DR, Link Type, Cost, Submit URL, Approval Time, Best For and Tips. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets, filter by relevance, verify publishers and track your own submissions. Recorded values are not live measurements. Third-party costs, acceptance and rankings are not guaranteed.

[Download a real five-row sample](https://backlinkgrid.com/samples/backlink-database-sample.csv).
