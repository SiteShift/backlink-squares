Every industry has unique link building opportunities and challenges. What works for a SaaS company differs from what works for a local restaurant or a healthcare provider. These guides provide tailored strategies for specific verticals.

## Why Industry Matters

### Different Opportunities

Each industry offers unique link sources:
- **SaaS**: Integration directories, tech publications
- **Healthcare**: Medical sites, health publications (with strict requirements)
- **Local business**: Citations, community sites, local press
- **Finance**: Authority requirements, regulatory considerations

### Different Challenges

Industries face different obstacles:
- **YMYL sectors**: Higher trust requirements
- **Local businesses**: Geographic relevance focus
- **B2B**: Longer sales cycles, niche audiences
- **E-commerce**: Product page links, competitive markets

### Different Competitors

Understanding your specific competitive landscape matters:
- Who ranks in your space
- What links your competitors have
- What works in your niche

## Choosing Your Strategy

### Assess Your Industry

**High competition**: Need unique angles, higher investment
**Low competition**: Basic tactics may work

### Match Tactics to Vertical

**Data-heavy industries**: Original research works well
**Visual industries**: Infographics, galleries
