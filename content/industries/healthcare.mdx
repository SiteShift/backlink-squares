Healthcare is the definitive YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) category. Link building in this space requires exceptional attention to authority, accuracy, and trust. This guide covers strategies for medical and health-related businesses.

## Healthcare Link Building Reality

### YMYL Requirements

Google applies highest scrutiny to health content:
- Medical accuracy required
- Expert authorship expected
- Trust signals heavily weighted
- Poor information carries real harm risk

### E-E-A-T is Essential

**Experience**: Real medical/healthcare experience

**Expertise**: Medical credentials, qualifications

**Authoritativeness**: Recognition from medical community

**Trustworthiness**: Accuracy, transparency, security

### What This Means for Link Building

- Links from medical authorities carry exceptional weight
- Non-authoritative links may provide less value
- Building medical E-E-A-T supports all SEO efforts
- Quality far outweighs quantity

### Professional Credentials

- Board certifications displayed

- Reviewer credentials displayed

### Institutional Authority

**Organisational credibility**:

- Certifications held
- Industry memberships

## Healthcare Compliance

### Content Compliance

**Be aware of**:
- HIPAA (patient privacy)
- FDA regulations (claims about treatments)
- FTC guidelines (testimonials, endorsements)
- State medical board regulations

### Testimonial Caution

**Patient testimonials**:
- Consent required
- Cannot imply guaranteed outcomes
- May have regulatory restrictions
- Use carefully or avoid

### Claims and Disclaimers

**Medical claims**:
- Must be substantiated
- Should align with evidence
- Need appropriate disclaimers
- Require regular review

## Common Healthcare Mistakes

### Underestimating YMYL Impact

**Problem**: Treating healthcare like any other industry

**Solution**: Recognise elevated standards apply

### Missing Medical Credentials

**Problem**: Content without author expertise signals

**Solution**: Attribute to qualified professionals

### Outdated Medical Information

**Problem**: Content with outdated medical guidance

**Solution**: Regular review and update process

### Ignoring Compliance

**Problem**: Claims or content that violate regulations

**Solution**: Legal/compliance review for content
