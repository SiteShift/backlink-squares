Local businesses need links that demonstrate geographic relevance and community presence. This guide covers strategies for businesses serving specific locations—restaurants, professional services, retailers, and other locally-focused companies.

## Local Link Building Landscape

### Unique Opportunities

**Local citations**: Business directories with NAP consistency

**Community involvement**: Local organisations and events

**Local press**: Regional news and publications

**Hyperlocal content**: Location-specific resources

**Neighbouring businesses**: Local business relationships

### Unique Focus Areas

**Geographic relevance**: Links need local signals

**NAP consistency**: Name, Address, Phone uniformity

**Local authority**: Community trust building

**Map pack optimisation**: Google Business Profile support

## Local Citations

### What They Are

Business listings on directories and aggregators:
- Google Business Profile
- Yelp, TripAdvisor
- Yellow Pages, White Pages
- Industry-specific directories
- Local business directories

### Core Citations

**Essential directories** (prioritise these first):
- Google Business Profile
- Yelp
- Facebook Business
- Apple Maps
- Bing Places
- Better Business Bureau

**Data aggregators** (feed other directories):
- Infogroup
- Acxiom
- Localeze
- Factual

### Building Citations

**Process**:
1. Audit existing citations
2. Claim and optimise core listings
3. Submit to relevant directories
4. Ensure NAP consistency
5. Add rich content (photos, descriptions)

### NAP Consistency

**Critical**: Name, Address, Phone must match exactly everywhere

**Inconsistencies hurt**:
- Different business name formats
- Old addresses
- Tracking phone numbers
- Abbreviation differences

## Google Business Profile Optimisation

### The Foundation

Google Business Profile is central to local SEO:
- Primary listing for map pack
- Source of citations elsewhere
- Review platform
- Local authority signal

### Optimisation Checklist

- [ ] Complete all information fields
- [ ] Accurate NAP
- [ ] Primary and secondary categories
- [ ] Business description with keywords
- [ ] Regular photo uploads
- [ ] Respond to all reviews
- [ ] Regular posts and updates
- [ ] Products/services listed
- [ ] Q&A section addressed

### Connection to Link Building

GBP strengthens the impact of local links by:
- Establishing geographic authority
- Confirming business legitimacy
- Supporting local relevance signals

## Common Local Business Mistakes

### Inconsistent NAP

**Problem**: Different business info across listings

**Solution**: Audit and standardise all citations

### Ignoring Small Local Sites

**Problem**: Only focusing on major directories

**Solution**: Local relevance from small sites matters

### No Community Involvement

**Problem**: Just chasing links without genuine presence

**Solution**: Authentically participate in community

### Over-Optimising Local Content

**Problem**: Keyword-stuffed local pages

**Solution**: Create genuinely useful local resources

Local business link building focuses on geographic relevance:
