E-commerce sites face unique link building challenges—product pages are hard to get links to, category pages need authority, and competition is fierce. This guide covers strategies tailored to online retailers.

## E-commerce Link Building Landscape

### Unique Challenges

**Product pages**: Hard to earn links to commercial pages

**Scale**: Thousands of pages needing authority

**Competition**: Battling major retailers

**Transactional intent**: Most pages are commercial

**Product turnover**: Inventory changes break links

### Unique Opportunities

**Supplier relationships**: Manufacturer links

**Product uniqueness**: Exclusive or unique products

**Buying guides**: Informational content opportunities

**Brand partnerships**: Retailer-brand collaborations

**User-generated content**: Reviews and community

---

## Manufacturer and Supplier Links

### The Opportunity

Product manufacturers often list authorised retailers:
- "Where to buy" pages
- Authorised dealer directories
- Retailer finder tools

### How to Get Them

**For products you sell**:
1. Identify manufacturers you're authorised to sell
2. Find their retailer/dealer listings
3. Request inclusion
4. Provide required information

**Maximise opportunities**:
- Apply to all relevant manufacturer programs
- Keep listings updated
- Pursue premium/featured placement

### Building Supplier Relationships

**Strengthen relationships**:
- Become a preferred retailer
- Meet volume thresholds
- Provide good customer experience
- Participate in co-marketing

---

## Product Roundup and Gift Guide Links

### The Opportunity

Journalists and bloggers create:
- Gift guides (holiday, occasions)
- Best-of lists (best X for Y)
- Product roundups (top 10 lists)
- Buyer's guides

### How to Get Included

**Outreach approach**:
1. Identify relevant publications and writers
2. Monitor for roundup creation (seasonal timing)
3. Pitch products with angles and unique selling points
4. Provide high-quality images and information

**What makes products roundup-worthy**:
- Unique features or benefits
- Strong reviews/ratings
- Competitive pricing
- Interesting story or angle

### Timing Considerations

**Gift guides**: Pitch 2-3 months before holidays

**Seasonal roundups**: Know the content calendar

**Evergreen lists**: Ongoing opportunity

## Content-Driven Link Building

### Buying Guides

**Create comprehensive guides**:
- How to choose [product category]
- [Product] buying guide
- What to look for in [product]

**Why they earn links**:
- Genuinely useful information
- Objective buyer advice
- Citable resource

### Educational Content

**Topics that work**:
- Product care and maintenance
- Usage tutorials and tips
- Style guides and inspiration
- Industry knowledge

**Example**: A furniture store creating interior design guides, or a camera retailer publishing photography tutorials.

### Research and Data

**E-commerce data opportunities**:
- Pricing trends and analysis
- Consumer behaviour studies
- Product popularity data
- Industry statistics

---

## Category Page Authority

### The Challenge

Category pages are critical for SEO but hard to get links to directly.

### Internal Link Strategy

**Support category pages through**:
- Blog posts linking to categories
- Buying guides referencing categories
- Related content pointing to category
- Strategic internal linking

### Indirect Link Building

**Build links to content that links to categories**:
- Buying guides → Category pages
- How-to content → Product/category pages
- Resource content → Commercial pages

### Direct Category Links

**Occasional opportunities**:
- Resource pages listing shopping categories
- Industry directories
- Comparison sites

## E-commerce Specific Tactics

### Scholarship Links (.edu)

**Approach** (use cautiously):
- Create genuine scholarships
- Apply to .edu scholarship databases
- Ensure authentic intent

**Note**: This tactic is saturated and viewed sceptically. Only pursue if genuinely committed to supporting students.

### Product Donation Links

**Strategy**:
- Donate products to organisations
- Sponsor events with product prizes
- Support charity auctions

**Results**:
- Links from organisation websites
- Event sponsor pages
- Thank-you mentions

### Coupon and Deal Sites

**Consideration**:
- Usually nofollow links
- Traffic value over link value
- Can drive sales

**Approach**:
- Submit deals to legitimate coupon sites
- Create exclusive deals for partners
- Monitor brand mentions on deal sites

## Common E-commerce Mistakes

### Ignoring Content

**Problem**: All commercial pages, no content strategy

**Solution**: Build informational content that supports commercial goals

### Chasing Generic Links

**Problem**: Links from irrelevant sites

**Solution**: Focus on industry-relevant opportunities

### Neglecting Manufacturer Relationships

**Problem**: Missing easy link opportunities

**Solution**: Systematically pursue all supplier link programs

### No Link Maintenance

**Problem**: Products discontinued, links broken

**Solution**: Redirect discontinued products, maintain link value

## Summary

E-commerce link building requires working around commercial page limitations:

**Key strategies**:
- Manufacturer and supplier links
- Product roundup and gift guide inclusion
- Content-driven link building
- Internal linking to support categories
- Brand building for natural links

**Focus areas**:
- Build relationships with suppliers
- Create genuinely useful buying guides
- Monitor roundup and gift guide opportunities
- Support commercial pages with linked content

**Success factors**:
- Unique products or angles
- Quality content alongside commerce
- Strong supplier relationships
- Brand worth talking about

Build content and relationships that earn links, then strategically connect that value to your commercial pages.
