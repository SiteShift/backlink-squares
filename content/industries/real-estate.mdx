Real estate is inherently local, making geographic relevance crucial for link building. This guide covers strategies for real estate agents, brokers, and property companies.

## Real Estate Link Building Landscape

### Local Focus

Real estate is location-specific:
- Market areas define competition
- Local authority matters most
- Geographic content opportunities
- Community relationships drive business

### Trust Requirements

Buying property is a major decision:
- Agent credibility essential
- Local expertise valued
- Trust signals matter
- Professional authority needed

### Competition Dynamics

**Local competition**: Multiple agents per market
**Portal dominance**: Zillow, Realtor.com, etc.
**Personal branding**: Agent vs brokerage focus

## Local Authority Building

### Geographic Content

**Neighbourhood and area content**:
- Neighbourhood guides
- School information
- Local amenities
- Market statistics
- Community profiles

**Why it earns links**:
- Genuinely useful local information
- Referenced by relocators and residents
- Shared by community organisations

### Local Market Data

**Data-driven content**:
- Market reports (monthly/quarterly)
- Price trend analysis
- Inventory reports
- Market comparisons

**Distribution**:
- Local media picks up data
- Other agents/sites reference
- Creates citation opportunities

## Common Real Estate Mistakes

**Problem**: Generic real estate content

**Solution**: Hyperlocal, area-specific content

### Neglecting Community Involvement

**Problem**: No local presence beyond listings

**Solution**: Genuine community participation

**Problem**: Indistinguishable from other agents
