Travel is a content-rich industry with abundant link building opportunities—destination content, visual assets, and passionate audiences. This guide covers strategies for travel businesses, from hotels to tour operators.

## Travel Link Building Landscape

### Unique Opportunities

**Content-rich industry**: Endless destination and experience topics

**Visual appeal**: Photography and video opportunities

**Engaged audiences**: Travellers love sharing and discovering

**Media coverage**: Extensive travel journalism

**Influencer ecosystem**: Travel bloggers and creators

### Competitive Challenges

**Major player dominance**: OTAs, travel aggregators

**Seasonal variation**: Demand fluctuates

**Review dependence**: TripAdvisor, Google reviews critical

**Content saturation**: Many creating similar content

---

## Destination Content Strategy

### Creating Destination Guides

**Comprehensive destination content**:
- Complete city/region guides
- Itinerary suggestions
- Local tips and insights
- Practical travel information

**What makes guides link-worthy**:
- More comprehensive than competitors
- Genuine local knowledge
- Current information
- Unique perspectives

### Content Types

**Destination content that earns links**:
- "[Destination] travel guide"
- "Things to do in [destination]"
- "[Destination] itinerary (3 days, 1 week, etc.)"
- "Hidden gems in [destination]"
- "[Destination] for [audience type]"

### Local Partnership Content

**Collaborate with local businesses**:
- Restaurant and food guides
- Local attraction features
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Local expert interviews

## Common Travel Mistakes

### Generic Destination Content

**Problem**: Same content as everyone else

**Solution**: Unique angles, local expertise, deeper information

### Ignoring Visual Opportunities

**Problem**: Not leveraging travel's visual appeal

**Solution**: Invest in photography, video, visual content

### Seasonal Neglect

**Problem**: Not timing content with travel planning cycles

**Solution**: Content calendar aligned with travel seasons

### Review Platform Neglect

**Problem**: Ignoring TripAdvisor and review sites

**Solution**: Active reputation management
