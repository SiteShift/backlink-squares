## Building Credibility First

### Trust Signals

**Establish legitimacy**:
- Team transparency (real people, real backgrounds)
- Company registration and legal structure
- Security audits (for protocols/platforms)
- Regulatory compliance (where applicable)
- Clear communication

### Differentiating from Scams

**Demonstrate legitimacy through**:
- Transparent team information
- Clear use cases and business models
- No unrealistic return promises
- Honest market positioning
- Verifiable claims

### E-E-A-T for Crypto

**Experience**: Real industry experience demonstrated

**Expertise**: Technical and financial knowledge

**Authoritativeness**: Industry recognition

**Trustworthiness**: Transparency, honesty, security

## Avoiding Crypto Link Pitfalls

### What to Avoid

**Paid crypto links**: Many crypto "news" sites sell links—these are typically low quality and risky

**Link schemes**: Crypto-specific PBNs and link networks

**Spam tactics**: Forum spam, comment spam in crypto communities

**Misleading content**: Overpromising returns, deceptive content

### Red Flags in Crypto Links

**Warning signs**:
- Sites that only publish paid content
- "Press release" sites with no editorial
- Networks of similar crypto sites
- Sites with obvious paid placements

**Problem**: Not establishing legitimacy first

**Solution**: Build trust foundation before aggressive marketing

### Hype Over Substance

**Problem**: Marketing speak without genuine value
