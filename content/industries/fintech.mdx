Fintech companies operate at the intersection of finance and technology—an industry requiring exceptional trust and authority. This guide covers link building strategies for financial technology companies.

## Fintech Link Building Landscape

### YMYL Considerations

Fintech falls under "Your Money or Your Life" content:
- Higher trust standards from Google
- E-E-A-T signals matter more
- Authority requirements elevated
- Content scrutiny increased

### Unique Challenges

**Trust requirements**: Users and search engines demand credibility

**Regulatory environment**: Compliance affects content and claims

**Competition**: Established finance and emerging fintech

**Scepticism**: New finance products face doubt

### Unique Opportunities

**Tech ecosystem**: Integration and partnership opportunities

**Finance publications**: Extensive coverage of fintech

**Data stories**: Financial data creates compelling content

**Innovation narrative**: Media interest in disruption

## Authority Building Fundamentals

### E-E-A-T for Fintech

**Experience**: Demonstrate real-world financial experience

**Expertise**: Credentials, qualifications, knowledge

**Authoritativeness**: Industry recognition, coverage, citations

**Trustworthiness**: Security, compliance, transparency

### Building Trust Signals

**Credentials and compliance**:
- Regulatory licences displayed
- Security certifications
- Industry memberships
- Compliance statements

**Team authority**:
- Executive backgrounds highlighted
- Financial expertise demonstrated
- Advisory board showcased

**Social proof**:
- Customer testimonials
- Case studies
- Reviews and ratings
- Industry awards

## Common Fintech Mistakes

### Ignoring E-E-A-T

**Problem**: Not demonstrating expertise and trust

**Solution**: Build authority signals systematically

### Aggressive Claims

**Problem**: Marketing language that can't be substantiated

**Solution**: Fact-based, compliant content

### Only Chasing Tech Links

**Problem**: Neglecting finance authority

**Solution**: Balance tech and finance coverage

### Underestimating Trust Requirements

**Problem**: Treating fintech like regular tech

**Solution**: Recognise elevated trust standards
