**NAP consistency** refers to keeping your business's Name, Address, and Phone number identical across all online mentions, directories, and platforms. Inconsistent NAP information confuses search engines and can negatively impact local search rankings.

## What Is NAP

### The Components

**N - Name**:
- Your exact business name
- Legal name vs DBA
- Consistent formatting

**A - Address**:
- Physical business address
- Suite/unit numbers
- Consistent abbreviations

**P - Phone**:
- Primary phone number
- Format consistency
- Tracking numbers considerations

---

## Why NAP Consistency Matters

### Search Engine Trust

**Consistent NAP signals**:
- Verified business information
- Legitimacy indicators
- Reliable data
- Trustworthy entity

### Local SEO Impact

**Inconsistency causes**:
- Ranking difficulties
- Confused local signals
- Diluted authority
- Potential duplicate issues

### User Experience

**For customers**:
- Correct contact information
- Accurate locations
- Reliable business data
- Trust in business

---

## Common NAP Inconsistencies

### Name Variations

**Examples**:
- "John's Plumbing" vs "Johns Plumbing"
- "ABC Inc." vs "ABC Incorporated"
- "The Widget Shop" vs "Widget Shop"
- "&" vs "and"

### Address Variations

**Examples**:
- "Street" vs "St."
- "Suite" vs "Ste." vs "#"
- "Road" vs "Rd."
- Missing unit numbers
- Old addresses

### Phone Variations

**Examples**:
- (555) 123-4567 vs 555-123-4567
- 555.123.4567 vs 5551234567
- Local vs 800 numbers
- Tracking numbers

## Achieving NAP Consistency

### Step 1: Establish Your Standard

**Define exactly**:
- Precise business name
- Exact address format
- Phone number format
- Use this everywhere

### Step 2: Audit Existing Citations

**Find inconsistencies**:
- Search for your business
- Use citation tracking tools
- Check all directories
- Document variations

### Step 3: Correct Issues

**Fix problems**:
- Update incorrect listings
- Claim and correct profiles
- Remove duplicates
- Standardize all entries

### Step 4: Maintain Consistency

**Ongoing**:
- Use standard for new listings
- Regular audits
- Update for any changes
- Monitor for new issues

## Handling Special Cases

### Multiple Locations

**For each location**:
- Unique address
- Location-specific phone
- Consistent name formatting
- Separate profiles

### Name Changes

**When rebranding**:
- Update all listings
- Use redirects if needed
- Be thorough and complete
- Monitor for old references

### Address Changes

**When moving**:
- Update everywhere simultaneously
- Check for old references
- Update Google Business Profile first
- Verify new listings

### Phone Number Changes

**When changing numbers**:
- Update all platforms
- Consider forwarding
- Monitor for old references
- Prioritize key directories
