**A contributor post** is content written by a regular contributor to a publication, rather than a one-time guest author. Contributors typically have an ongoing relationship with the publication and publish multiple articles over time, often with dedicated author pages and profiles.

## Contributor Posts vs Guest Posts

### Key Differences

| Aspect | Guest Post | Contributor Post |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| Frequency | One-time | Ongoing/regular |
| Relationship | Transactional | Partnership |
| Author profile | Minimal | Dedicated page |
| Editorial access | Per submission | Often streamlined |
| Authority building | Limited | Substantial |

### Contributor Advantages

**Benefits over guest posting**:
- Multiple link opportunities
- Stronger author profile
- Platform for authority
- Streamlined publishing
- Deeper relationships

## Becoming a Contributor

### The Path

**Typical progression**:
1. Guest post successfully
2. Build relationship with editors
3. Pitch for regular column
4. Establish ongoing arrangement
5. Publish consistently

### What Publications Want

**Ideal contributors**:
- Consistent quality
- Original perspectives
- Reliable delivery
- Subject matter expertise
- Engaged with their audience

## Pitching to Become a Contributor

### Initial Approach

**After guest posting**:
```
Subject: Becoming a regular contributor

Hi [Editor],

Thanks again for publishing my piece on [topic].
The response from your readers was great.

I'd love to discuss becoming a regular contributor.
I have several more topic ideas that would fit your
audience well:

- [Topic idea 1]
- [Topic idea 2]
- [Topic idea 3]

Would you be open to an ongoing arrangement?

Best,
[Your name]
```

### What to Offer

**Your pitch should convey**:
- Your expertise
- Topic ideas pipeline
- Reliability
- Understanding of their audience
- Commitment to quality
