**Geo-targeted links** are backlinks from websites that have geographic relevance to your target area. These links help establish your business's authority and relevance within a specific geographic region, supporting local SEO efforts.

## Understanding Geo-Targeted Links

### What Makes a Link Geo-Targeted

**Geographic relevance from**:
- Local business websites
- Regional news outlets
- City/area-specific sites
- Local organization websites
- Regional directories

### How They Help

**Geo-targeted links signal**:
- Geographic relevance
- Local community involvement
- Regional authority
- Area-specific expertise

## Geo-Targeting Signals

### What Creates Geographic Relevance

**Signals include**:

- Audience in your region
- Local business/organization
- Regional domain extensions

### Strengthening Signals

- Anchor text with city/area
