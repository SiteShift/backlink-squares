**Curated links** are backlinks included in hand-selected, editorially-chosen resource collections. Unlike automated or algorithmic inclusions, curated links represent deliberate human decisions to recommend a resource, making them valuable for SEO and referral traffic.

## Understanding Curated Links

### What Makes Links "Curated"

**Curated links are**:
- Manually selected
- Editorially chosen
- Quality-filtered
- Contextually relevant
- Intentionally included

### Contrast with Automated

**Non-curated links**:
- Algorithmically generated
- Auto-submitted
- No human review
- Mass inclusion
- Lower quality signal

---

## Types of Curated Link Sources

### Resource Pages

**Hand-selected resources**:
- "Best tools for [topic]"
- "Recommended reading"
- "Helpful resources"
- Industry guides

### Link Roundups

**Regular curated posts**:
- Weekly/monthly roundups
- "Best of" compilations
- Curated newsletters
- Content digests

### Expert Recommendations

**Personal curation**:
- Influencer recommendations
- Expert tool lists
- Professional favorites
- Trusted resource lists

### Editorial Lists

**Publication curation**:
- Magazine "best of" lists
- Review site recommendations
- Industry publication picks
- Award lists

## Earning Curated Links

### Create Quality Content

**What gets curated**:
- Genuinely useful resources
- High-quality content
- Unique tools or data
- Comprehensive guides
- Original research

### Reach the Curators

**Outreach approaches**:
- Introduce your resource
- Demonstrate value
- Show relevance to their audience
- Make it easy to evaluate

### Build Relationships

**Long-term approach**:
- Engage with curators
- Share their content
- Provide value
- Become known quality source

## Getting Into Curated Collections

### Research Opportunities

**Find collections by**:
- Searching "[topic] resources"
- Analyzing competitor backlinks
- Following industry curators
- Monitoring roundups

### Evaluate Fit

**Good targets**:
- Relevant to your content
- Actively maintained
- Quality standards apparent
- Real audience
- Editorial discretion

### Pitch Effectively

**Outreach elements**:
- Specific reference to their collection
- Clear explanation of your resource
- Why it fits their audience
- Easy evaluation path

## Curated Links vs Other Link Types

### Comparison

| Type | Selection | Quality Signal | Effort |
|------|-----------|----------------|--------|
| Curated | Manual | High | Medium |
| Directory | Auto/paid | Low | Low |
| Editorial | Manual | Highest | High |
| Comment | Self-placed | Very low | Very low |
