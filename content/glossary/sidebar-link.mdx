**A sidebar link** is a hyperlink placed in the sidebar section of a webpage, typically appearing on every page of a website. Sidebars commonly contain navigation menus, recent posts, categories, blogrolls, or widget areas where links can be placed.

## How Sidebar Links Work

### Placement and Visibility

Sidebar links appear in the vertical column alongside main content:
- Usually on the left or right side of pages
- Often repeated across multiple or all pages
- May contain navigation, resources, or promotional links
- Commonly found in blog and content site layouts

### Common Sidebar Link Types

**Blogroll links**: Lists of recommended sites
**Widget links**: Links in sidebar widgets or plugins
**Resource links**: Links to tools or related resources
**Sponsor links**: Paid promotional placements
**Navigation links**: Internal site navigation

---

## SEO Impact of Sidebar Links

### Google's Treatment

Google's algorithms have evolved to understand sidebar links:

**Reduced weight**: Sidebar links often carry less SEO value than in-content links
**Sitewide consideration**: Google may count multiple instances as a single link
**Context evaluation**: Relevance of the sidebar to main content matters
**User experience signals**: How users interact with sidebar links affects value

### The Reasonable Surfer Model

Google's patent describes evaluating links based on likelihood of being clicked:
- Sidebar links may receive lower click probability scores
- Position and prominence affect assigned value
- Links buried in sidebars carry less weight than prominent ones

---

## Sidebar Links vs Contextual Links

### Key Differences

| Aspect | Sidebar Links | Contextual Links |
|--------|---------------|------------------|
| Placement | Side column | Within content |
| Editorial context | Limited | High |
| Click probability | Lower | Higher |
| SEO value | Reduced | Higher |
| Natural occurrence | Common | Most valuable |

### Why Context Matters

Contextual links within content:
- Have clear topical relevance
- Are more likely to be clicked
- Suggest editorial endorsement
- Pass more link equity

---

## When Sidebar Links Are Acceptable

### Legitimate Uses

**Resource lists**: Curated industry resources
**Partner links**: Legitimate business partnerships
**Navigation**: Helping users find related content
**Blogrolls**: Editorial recommendations (less common now)

### Natural Sidebar Links

Natural sidebar links typically:
- Come from genuine relationships
- Serve user needs
- Aren't paid placements
- Appear on relevant sites

---

## Sidebar Link Risks

### Potential Problems

**Sitewide links**: Creating hundreds of links from one domain
**Link schemes**: Paid sidebar placements for SEO
**Widget abuse**: Links embedded in free widgets
**Irrelevant sites**: Sidebar links from unrelated niches

### Google's Guidelines

Google has specifically addressed sidebar links:
- Widget links should use nofollow
- Paid sidebar placements need disclosure
- Manipulative sidebar link schemes violate guidelines
- Excessive sitewide linking appears unnatural

---

## Best Practices for Sidebar Links

### If You're Getting Sidebar Links

**Prefer contextual**: Ask for in-content placement instead
**Check relevance**: Ensure the site is topically related
**Verify quality**: Assess the linking site's authority
**Consider nofollow**: May be more appropriate for sidebar placement

### If You're Giving Sidebar Links

**Use nofollow**: For paid or uncertain links
**Limit quantity**: Don't create excessive outbound sidebar links
**Stay relevant**: Only link to related, quality resources
**Review regularly**: Audit sidebar links periodically

---

## Sidebar Links in Modern SEO

### Declining Importance

Sidebar links have become less important because:
- Search engines devalue them
- Users often ignore sidebars (banner blindness)
- Mobile-first indexing reduces sidebar visibility
- Contextual links are clearly more valuable

### When They Still Matter

Sidebar links can still provide value:
- Referral traffic from relevant sites
- Brand visibility
- Relationship building
- User navigation (internal links)

---

## Summary

Sidebar links are hyperlinks placed in webpage sidebars:

**Key characteristics**:
- Appear in side columns
- Often sitewide
- Lower SEO value than contextual links
- May be discounted by Google

**Best practices**:
- Prefer contextual links when possible
- Use nofollow for paid placements
- Focus on relevance and quality
- Don't rely on sidebar links for SEO

While sidebar links aren't inherently harmful, they're less valuable than editorial contextual links and should be approached carefully in link building.
