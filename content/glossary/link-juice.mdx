**Link juice** is an informal SEO term referring to the value or authority passed from one page to another through hyperlinks. When a page links to another page, it passes some of its ranking power to the linked page.

## Understanding Link Juice

### The Concept

**How it works**:
- Pages accumulate authority
- Links pass some of that authority
- Linked pages receive a boost
- The "juice" flows through links

### The Metaphor

**Think of it like**:
- Water flowing through pipes
- Electricity through wires
- Influence spreading through connections
- Value being shared

---

## How Link Juice Flows

### External Links

**From other sites**:
- Other sites link to your pages
- Their authority flows to you
- Higher authority = more juice
- Quality and relevance matter

### Internal Links

**Within your site**:
- Your pages link to each other
- Authority distributes internally
- You control internal flow
- Strategic linking matters

### Outbound Links

**To other sites**:
- Your links pass juice outward
- Some value leaves your site
- But linking out is natural
- Don't hoard links unnaturally

---

## Factors Affecting Link Juice

### Passing Page Authority

**More juice from**:
- High authority pages
- Well-linked pages
- Relevant pages
- Trusted domains

### Number of Links

**Dilution effect**:
- Juice splits among all links
- More links = less per link
- Pages with few links pass more
- Balance is important

### Link Position

**Location matters**:
- Editorial/content links pass more
- Footer/sidebar links pass less
- First links may matter more
- Context affects value

### Follow Status

**Nofollow impact**:
- Nofollow links don't pass juice (traditionally)
- Now treated as "hints" by Google
- Dofollow passes full value
- Most links should be natural

---

## Link Juice vs Link Equity

### The Terms

**Essentially synonymous**:
- "Link juice" = informal/colloquial
- "Link equity" = professional/technical
- Same concept, different language
- Link equity is preferred in formal contexts

### Modern Understanding

**Current thinking**:
- Value flows through links
- Multiple factors affect transfer
- Not just PageRank anymore
- Complex algorithm considerations

## Maximizing Link Juice

### External Link Acquisition

**Get more juice by**:
- Earning links from high-authority sites
- Building relevant links
- Acquiring links to strategic pages
- Creating linkable content

### Internal Link Optimization

## Internal Link Juice Strategy

### Hub and Spoke Model

**Structure**:
- Central hub pages receive links
- They link to related pages
- Creates topical clusters
- Distributes authority effectively

### Homepage Distribution

**Leverage homepage authority**:
- Homepage often has most juice
- Link to priority pages
- Don't waste on low-value pages
- Balance user needs with SEO

### Deep Linking

**Reach deep pages**:
- Ensure internal links reach all pages
- Avoid orphan pages
- Create logical paths
- Maintain crawlability

- Editorial placements

### Relevance Matters

**Juice flows better when**:
- Topics are related
- Context is appropriate
- Link makes sense
- Audience overlap exists

**Juice accumulates**:
- Older links may pass more
- Trust builds over time
- Consistent linking helps
- Stability matters

## Common Misconceptions

### Myth: You Can Precisely Calculate Juice

**Reality**: Juice flow is complex and not precisely calculable. Focus on best practices rather than exact calculations.

### Myth: All Links Pass Equal Juice

**Reality**: Link value varies significantly based on many factors including authority, relevance, and placement.

### Myth: Linking Out Wastes Your Juice

**Reality**: Natural outbound linking is healthy. Excessive hoarding looks unnatural and may harm you.

### Myth: Nofollow Links Are Worthless

**Reality**: They can still drive traffic, brand mentions, and may pass some signals as Google treats nofollow as a "hint."
