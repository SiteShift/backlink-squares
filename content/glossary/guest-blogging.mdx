**Guest blogging** (or guest posting) is the practice of writing and publishing content on another website. For link building, guest posts typically include a backlink to your site, either in the author bio or within the content itself.

## How Guest Blogging Works

### The Exchange

**Value for both parties**:
- Host site: Free quality content
- Guest author: Backlink + exposure + authority

### The Process

1. Find relevant guest post opportunities
2. Pitch topic ideas to the site
3. Write quality content if accepted
4. Include appropriate backlinks
5. Get published and promoted

### Qualifying Sites

**Good guest post targets**:
- Relevant to your niche
- Quality content standards
- Active, engaged audience
- Reasonable domain authority
- Not "pay-to-post" schemes

## Pitching Guest Posts

### Research First

**Before pitching**:
- Read their existing content
- Understand their audience
- Check their guidelines
- Find content gaps
- Prepare relevant ideas

### Pitch Structure

**Effective pitch**:
1. Brief introduction
2. Why you/why them
3. 2-3 specific topic ideas
4. Brief outline for each
5. Relevant credentials
6. Writing samples

### Example Pitch

```
Subject: Guest post idea: [Specific Topic]

Hi [Name],

I've been reading [Blog Name] for a while—especially
enjoyed your recent post on [specific post].

I'd love to contribute a guest post for your readers.
Based on what you typically publish, I think one of
these might work well:

1. [Topic 1] - [Brief description, ~20 words]
2. [Topic 2] - [Brief description, ~20 words]
3. [Topic 3] - [Brief description, ~20 words]

A bit about me: [1-2 sentences on relevant credentials]

Here are some examples of my writing: [1-2 links]

Let me know if any of these interest you, or if you
have a different topic in mind.

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Writing Quality Guest Posts

### Content Standards

**Quality guest posts**:
- Match host site's quality
- Provide genuine value
- Are well-researched
- Include original insights
- Follow their guidelines

### Link Placement

**Types of links**:
- Author bio link (most common)
- Contextual links within content
- Resource citations

**Best practices**:
- Follow site guidelines
- Don't over-link
- Use natural anchors
- Link to genuinely helpful content

---

## Guest Blogging Best Practices

### Do

- Research sites thoroughly
- Pitch unique, quality ideas
- Write your best content
- Follow their guidelines
- Promote the published post
- Build ongoing relationships

### Don't

- Pitch generic ideas
- Submit low-quality content
- Stuff with promotional links
- Ignore their guidelines
- Ghost after publication
- Treat it purely transactionally

---

## Google's View on Guest Posting

### The Guidelines

**Google's stance**:
- Quality guest posts are fine
- Scaled, low-quality campaigns are not
- Links should be editorial
- Excessive keyword-rich anchors are problematic

### What to Avoid

**Problematic practices**:
- Mass low-quality guest posting
- Paid guest post schemes
- Keyword-stuffed anchor text
- Duplicate/spun content
- Posting on irrelevant sites

### Safe Guest Blogging

**Stay safe by**:
- Focusing on quality over quantity
- Writing genuinely valuable content
- Using natural anchor text
- Choosing relevant, quality sites
- Not scaling excessively

## Guest Post Links

### Link Types

**Common arrangements**:
- **Bio link**: Link in author bio (safest)
- **Contextual link**: Link within content
- **Resource link**: Link as a citation
- **Combination**: Multiple link types

### Anchor Text

**Best practices**:
- Branded anchors common in bios
- Natural, varied anchors in content
- Avoid exact match keywords
- Match the context naturally
