**Connectively** is a platform connecting journalists with expert sources, positioned as the evolution of services like HARO. It allows experts to respond to journalist queries and potentially earn backlinks when quoted in articles from major publications.

## Understanding Connectively

### What It Is

**Platform purpose**:
- Connects journalists with sources
- Facilitates expert commentary
- Enables earned media opportunities
- Successor to HARO-style services

### How It Works

**Basic process**:
1. Create a source profile
2. Browse or receive journalist queries
3. Submit relevant responses
4. Get quoted in articles (when selected)
5. Earn links from attribution

### Query Discovery

**How to find queries**:
- Browse open opportunities
- Email notifications
- Filter by category
- Search by keyword
- Check deadlines

### Evaluating Queries

**Good opportunities**:
- Match your genuine expertise
- From quality publications
- Clear requirements
- Reasonable timeline
- Worth your time

## Responding Effectively

### Response Best Practices

**For success**:
- Answer the actual question
- Be concise and quotable
- Show genuine expertise
- Respond quickly
- Follow their format requirements

### Response Structure

**Effective format**:
1. Direct answer
2. Supporting context
3. Credentials
4. Contact for follow-up

### What Journalists Need

**Provide**:
- Specific, quotable statements
- Unique perspectives
- Real-world examples
- Verifiable expertise
- Timely responses

---

## Competition and Conversion

### The Reality

**Expect**:
- Many responses per query
- Low selection rates (5-15%)
- Some queries get hundreds of responses
- Quality matters enormously

### Improving Your Odds

**Stand out by**:
- Responding early
- Providing unique angles
- Being genuinely qualified
- Formatting professionally
- Adding specific value

---

## Links and Attribution

### How Links Happen

**When quoted, you may receive**:
- Contextual link in article
- Link in author bio/attribution
- Mention without link
- Link with nofollow
- Various anchor text

### Not Guaranteed

**Understand that**:
- Not all quotes include links
- Some publications don't link
- Nofollow is common
- Mentions still have value
