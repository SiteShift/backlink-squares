**Ego bait** is content specifically designed to appeal to the egos of influencers, experts, or brands in order to earn links, shares, and mentions from them. By featuring, quoting, or positively recognizing someone, you increase the likelihood they'll link to and promote your content.

## How Ego Bait Works

### The Psychology

**Why it works**:
- People enjoy recognition
- Being featured is flattering
- Natural impulse to share
- Pride in being included
- Reciprocity principle

### The Mechanism

1. Create content featuring someone
2. Notify them of the feature
3. They're flattered by inclusion
4. They link to or share it
5. Their audience sees it too

---

## Types of Ego Bait Content

### Expert Roundups

**Format**: Collect insights from multiple experts
**Appeal**: Featured as industry expert
**Example**: "50 SEO Experts Share Their Top Tips"

### Best-Of Lists

**Format**: Curate top people/companies
**Appeal**: Recognition and ranking
**Example**: "Top 25 Marketing Influencers to Follow"

### Interviews

**Format**: In-depth feature on individual
**Appeal**: Platform and exposure
**Example**: "Interview: How [Person] Built Their Business"

### Awards and Recognition

**Format**: Create awards or recognition
**Appeal**: Prestige and validation
**Example**: "Industry Excellence Awards Winners"

### Quotes and Citations

**Format**: Quote experts in content
**Appeal**: Cited as authority
**Example**: Including expert quotes throughout articles

### Case Studies

**Format**: Feature company success
**Appeal**: Positive publicity
**Example**: "How [Company] Achieved [Result]"

## Creating Effective Ego Bait

### Selection Criteria

**Choose subjects who**:
- Have websites (can link)
- Are active online
- Share content regularly
- Have engaged audiences
- Are relevant to your niche

### Quality Standards

**Ego bait should be**:
- Genuinely valuable content
- Not obviously manipulative
- Fair and accurate
- Something to be proud of
- Worth their reputation

---

## Ethical Considerations

### Good Ego Bait

**Ethical characteristics**:
- Genuine recognition
- Accurate representation
- Quality content regardless
- Fair selection criteria
- Transparency

### Bad Ego Bait

**Avoid**:
- False or exaggerated praise
- Pay-to-be-featured schemes
- Manipulative intent obvious
- Low-quality content
- Clickbait titles

### The Line

**Ego bait is acceptable when**:
- Content would be valuable anyway
- Recognition is deserved
- You'd publish without links
- It helps your audience

**It becomes problematic when**:
- Sole purpose is manipulation
- Quality doesn't matter
- Recognition isn't deserved
- It's obviously fake

## Notification and Outreach

### Letting People Know

**Notification approaches**:
- Email: "Thought you'd like to know..."
- Social media: Tag them
- Keep it natural
- Don't demand anything

### Email Template

```
Subject: You're featured in our [content type]

Hi [Name],

I wanted to let you know that I included you in
our recent [article/roundup/list] on [topic].

Your [insight/work/contribution] on [specific thing]
was something I thought our readers would find
valuable.

Here's the link: [URL]

Thanks for the great work you do!

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Don't Be Pushy

**Avoid**:
- Asking for links explicitly
- Multiple follow-ups
- Demanding shares
- Making it transactional

## Success Factors

### What Increases Success

**Higher engagement when**:
- Quality of content is high
- Subject is active online
- Feature is genuinely positive
- Notification is casual
- Audience is relevant

### What Reduces Success

**Lower engagement when**:
- Obviously manufactured
- Low-quality content
- Pushy outreach
- Irrelevant inclusion
- Too many people featured

## Ego Bait Best Practices

### Do

- Create genuinely valuable content
- Feature people you actually respect
- Be accurate and fair
- Notify casually
- Make sharing easy
- Create something worth associating with

### Don't

- Create low-effort listicles
- Include people just for links
- Be manipulative in outreach
- Over-notify or pester
- Create fake awards
- Be obvious about intentions

## Ego Bait Variations

### Micro Ego Bait

**Approach**:
- Small mentions within content
- Quote one expert in article
- Link to their work
- Notify them
- Lower investment, lower return

### Macro Ego Bait

**Approach**:
- Entire piece dedicated to person
- In-depth interview or profile
- Significant feature
- Higher investment, higher return

### Community Ego Bait

**Approach**:
- Feature community members
- Customer spotlights
- User success stories
- Builds loyalty and links
