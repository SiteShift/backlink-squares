**Page Authority (PA)** is a metric developed by Moz that predicts how likely a specific page is to rank in search engine results. Scored from 0-100, Page Authority measures individual URL strength rather than entire domain authority.

## Page Authority vs Domain Authority

### Key Differences

| Page Authority (PA) | Domain Authority (DA) |
|--------------------|----------------------|
| Measures specific URL | Measures entire domain |
| Page-level prediction | Domain-level prediction |
| Based on links to that page | Based on links to domain |
| Varies across a site | Consistent for a domain |

### When to Use Each

**Use PA when**:
- Evaluating a specific page for link placement
- Assessing competing pages for a keyword
- Analysing page-level link building targets

**Use DA when**:
- Evaluating overall site quality
- Comparing domains
- Quick site-level assessment

---

## How Page Authority Works

### Calculation Factors

PA is calculated using:
- Links pointing to that specific URL
- Quality of those linking pages
- Moz's machine learning model
- Correlation with actual rankings

### The Score

**0-100 scale**: Higher = more likely to rank

**Logarithmic**: Harder to improve at higher levels

**Relative**: Best used for comparison, not absolute assessment

### What PA Reflects

PA indicates:
- Link strength of a specific page
- How much link equity that page has received
- Ranking potential for that URL

---

## Page Authority Ranges

### Typical Scores

| PA Range | Typical Pages |
|----------|---------------|
| 0-20 | New pages, thin content, few links |
| 20-40 | Established pages with some links |
| 40-60 | Strong pages, significant links |
| 60-80 | Very authoritative pages |
| 80-100 | Top pages (Wikipedia articles, major news) |

### Context Matters

A PA of 35 might be:
- Excellent for a small niche blog
- Average for a major publication
- Low for a critical business page

Always compare to competitors for the same keyword.

## Using Page Authority

### For Competitive Analysis

**Steps**:
1. Identify pages ranking for your target keyword
2. Check PA of top-ranking pages
3. Compare to your page's PA
4. Understand the gap to close

**Example**:
- Top 3 results have PA 45, 42, 38
- Your page has PA 25
- Significant link building needed

### For Link Building Targets

**Evaluating where to get links**:
- Higher PA pages pass more value
- PA indicates page-level link strength
- Consider alongside DA for full picture

**Good link source indicators**:
- Page PA appropriate for link value expected
- PA consistent with visible quality
- Combined with strong DA

### For Internal Linking

**Identify strong pages**:
- High PA pages on your site
- Use these to boost weaker pages
- Strategic internal link flow

---

## Improving Page Authority

### What Increases PA

**Build links to the specific page**:
- Each quality link increases PA
- Links to homepage don't directly boost inner pages
- Target link building to important pages

**Internal linking**:
- Links from high-PA pages on your site
- Strategic internal link architecture
- Support key pages with internal links

**Quality content**:
- Indirectly helps by attracting links
- Makes link building easier
- Increases link worthiness

### Common Mistakes

**Ignoring page-level links**: Only building homepage links

**Expecting DA to transfer**: DA doesn't automatically boost all pages

**Quick-fix expectations**: PA grows with sustained link building

## PA vs Other Page Metrics

### Comparison

| Metric | Provider | What It Measures |
|--------|----------|------------------|
| Page Authority | Moz | Page ranking potential |
| URL Rating | Ahrefs | Page link profile strength |
| Page Trust Flow | Majestic | Page trust from links |

### Which to Use

All measure similar concepts differently:
- Use whichever tool you prefer
- Consider using multiple for validation
- Don't obsess over exact numbers

---

## Page Authority Limitations

### What PA Isn't

**Not a Google metric**: Google doesn't use PA

**Not the only factor**: Rankings depend on many signals

**Not static**: Changes with algorithm updates and link changes

### Appropriate Use

**Good for**:
- Relative comparisons
- Competitive analysis
- Prospecting decisions
- Progress tracking

**Not good for**:
- Absolute ranking predictions
- Single-factor decisions
- Ignoring content/relevance

## Page Authority in Strategy

### Competitive Benchmarking

**Process**:
1. List target keywords
2. Check PA of ranking pages
3. Note the PA range for top positions
4. Set realistic targets

### Content Investment

**High-PA potential pages**:
- Already ranking content
- Link-worthy content
- Converting content
- Strategic priorities

Focus link building on pages where PA improvement yields results.

### Monitoring

Track PA for:
- Key money pages
- Top content pieces
- Pages you're actively building links to

## Summary

Page Authority is Moz's page-level ranking prediction:

**What it measures**:
- Individual URL strength
- Links pointing to that specific page
- Ranking potential for that URL

**How to use it**:
- Competitive analysis for keywords
- Evaluating link placement pages
- Tracking page-level progress
- Internal linking decisions

**Key points**:
- Different from Domain Authority
- Build links to specific pages to increase
- Use for comparison, not absolute prediction
- One factor among many

Focus on building page-level authority for your most important URLs.
