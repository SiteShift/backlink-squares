**An unlinked mention** is a reference to your brand, product, person, or content on another website that doesn't include a hyperlink. These mentions represent low-hanging fruit for link building because the site has already shown awareness and interest.

## Why Unlinked Mentions Matter

### Existing Relationship

**The advantage**:
- They already know you
- They chose to mention you
- Positive sentiment likely
- Easy conversion ask

### High Success Rate

**Compared to cold outreach**:
- Warmer relationship
- Natural request
- Lower friction
- Higher conversion

---

## Types of Unlinked Mentions

### Brand Mentions

**Your company name**:
- Company name in articles
- Brand references
- Business mentions

### Product Mentions

**Your products/services**:
- Product reviews without links
- Comparison articles
- Recommendation lists

### Personal Mentions

**People at your company**:
- Expert quotes
- Interview references
- Attribution without links

### Content Mentions

**Your content**:
- Studies cited without links
- Data referenced
- Guides mentioned

## Converting Mentions to Links

### Outreach Approach

**Key principles**:
- Thank them first
- Make the ask simple
- Provide the URL
- Don't demand

### Email Template

```
Subject: Thanks for the mention!

Hi [Name],

I just came across your article on [topic] and noticed
you mentioned [brand/product]. Thanks for including us!

Would you mind adding a link so your readers can easily
find us? Here's the URL: [URL]

No worries if not—I appreciate the mention either way!

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Follow-Up

**If no response**:
- Wait 5-7 days
- One follow-up maximum
- Keep it brief
- Accept the outcome

## Common Challenges

### Challenge: Negative Mentions

**Approach**:
- Don't request links
- Address the issue
- Focus on positive mentions

### Challenge: Old Content

**Approach**:
- Still worth trying
- Success rate may be lower
- Focus on recent mentions

### Challenge: No Contact Info

**Solutions**:
- Use contact forms
- Try social media
- Find via LinkedIn
- Use email finder tools
