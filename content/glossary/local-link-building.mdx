**Local link building** is the practice of acquiring backlinks from locally relevant sources to improve local search rankings. For businesses serving specific geographic areas, local links signal geographic relevance and build authority within the community.

## Why Local Links Matter

### Local SEO Impact

**Local links provide**:
- Geographic relevance signals
- Local authority building
- Community trust indicators
- Local pack ranking factors

### Beyond Citations

**Links complement citations**:
- Citations = NAP mentions
- Links = Authority signals
- Both matter for local SEO
- Links provide deeper value

## Local Link Building Strategies

### Scholarship Link Building

**Process**:
1. Create scholarship for local students
2. List on school websites
3. Earn .edu links
4. Build community goodwill

**Considerations**:
- Genuine scholarship (not just for links)
- Manageable commitment
- Real value to students

### Local Event Sponsorship

**Process**:
1. Identify local events
2. Become a sponsor
3. Earn sponsor page links
4. Get event website coverage

**Event types**:
- Sports teams/leagues
- Community festivals
- Charity events
- Professional conferences

### Local Resource Creation

**Create**:
- "Things to do in [City]" guides
- Local business directories
- Community event calendars
- Area guides for newcomers

**Promotion**:
- Share with local organizations
- Pitch to local media
- Offer as community resource

## Local Links vs National Links

### When Each Matters

**Local links best for**:
- Local pack rankings
- Geo-specific queries
- Community trust
- Local authority

**National links best for**:
- Overall domain authority
- Non-local queries
- Industry authority
- Broad visibility

### Balanced Approach

**Ideal strategy**:
- Strong local foundation
- Some national/industry links
- Citations for local SEO
- Quality over quantity
