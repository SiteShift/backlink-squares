**A business directory** is an online platform that lists businesses, typically with key information like name, address, phone number, website, and sometimes reviews. For local SEO, business directories are important sources of citations and can contribute to local search visibility.

## Understanding Business Directories

### What They Include

**Typical listing information**:
- Business name
- Address
- Phone number
- Website URL
- Hours of operation
- Description
- Categories
- Photos
- Reviews

### Types of Business Directories

**Major categories**:
- General directories (Yelp, Yellow Pages)
- Industry-specific directories
- Local/regional directories
- Review platforms
- Maps and navigation apps

---

## Business Directories and Local SEO

### Citation Importance

**Directories provide citations**:
- NAP (Name, Address, Phone) references
- Consistency signals to Google
- Local relevance indicators
- Trust signals

### Local Pack Factors

**Directory presence affects**:
- Local map rankings
- Business credibility
- Customer discovery
- Review aggregation

## Creating Quality Listings

### NAP Consistency

**Critical element**:
- Exact same name format
- Identical address format
- Consistent phone number
- Matching across all directories

### Complete Information

**Maximize listings with**:
- Full business description
- All relevant categories
- Accurate hours
- Quality photos
- Complete contact info

### Unique Descriptions

**For each directory**:
- Write unique descriptions
- Include relevant keywords
- Highlight differentiators
- Match audience expectations

---

## Business Directory Best Practices

### Claiming Listings

**Process**:
1. Search for existing listings
2. Claim unclaimed profiles
3. Verify ownership
4. Update information
5. Add enhancements

### Maintaining Listings

**Ongoing management**:
- Check accuracy regularly
- Update for changes
- Respond to reviews
- Add new photos
- Monitor for duplicates

---

## Directory Links vs Citations

### Links

**Direct SEO value**:
- Backlink to website
- Some directories provide dofollow
- Most are nofollow
- Limited direct link value

### Citations

**Local SEO value**:
- NAP mentions
- Consistency across web
- Trust signals
- Local relevance
