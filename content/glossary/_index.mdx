Understanding link building requires knowing the language. This glossary covers essential SEO and link building terms you'll encounter when building and analysing backlinks.

---

## A

### Anchor Text

The visible, clickable text in a hyperlink. Anchor text helps search engines understand what the linked page is about. Types include exact match, partial match, branded, naked URL, and generic.

[Full guide: Anchor Text →](/glossary/anchor-text)

### Authority

A measure of a website's or page's power and trustworthiness in search engines. Higher authority typically means better ranking potential. Various tools measure this differently (DA, DR, etc.).

### Alt Text Links

Links placed within image alt attributes. While technically functional, these provide less value than standard text links and are sometimes flagged as manipulative if overused.

---

## B

### Backlink

An incoming hyperlink from one website to another. Backlinks are a fundamental ranking factor because they act as votes of confidence from other sites. Also called inbound links or incoming links.

[Full guide: Backlink →](/glossary/backlink)

### Backlink Audit

The process of analysing your site's backlink profile to identify quality links, toxic links, and opportunities. Regular audits help maintain link profile health.

[Hub: Backlink Audit →](/backlink-audit)

### Backlink Gap

The difference between your backlink profile and competitors'—specifically, domains linking to competitors but not to you. Identifying gaps reveals link building opportunities.

### Backlink Profile

The complete collection of backlinks pointing to a website. A healthy profile has diverse, relevant, high-quality links from various sources and anchor texts.

### Black Hat SEO

Aggressive SEO techniques that violate search engine guidelines. In link building, this includes buying links, PBNs, and automated link schemes. Risks penalties.

[Full guide: Black Hat Link Building →](/link-building/avoid-black-hat)

### Blog Comment Links

Links placed in blog comments. While once effective for SEO, most are now nofollow and provide minimal link value. Can still drive referral traffic.

### Branded Anchor Text

Anchor text using a brand name (e.g., "Nike" or "Moz"). Important for natural link profiles as many organic links naturally use brand names.

### Broken Link

A hyperlink that leads to a non-existent page (404 error). Broken link building involves finding these and offering your content as a replacement.

[Tactic: Broken Link Building →](/link-building-tactics/broken-link-building)

---

## C

### Citation Flow

A Majestic metric measuring link quantity (how many sites link to a URL). Works with Trust Flow to evaluate link quality. High CF with low TF may indicate spam.

### Co-Citation

When two websites are mentioned together on a third site without being directly linked. Search engines may associate the two sites topically, even without direct links.

### Competitor Backlink Analysis

Researching which sites link to competitors to find potential link opportunities for your own site. A foundational prospecting technique.

[Full guide: Competitor Analysis →](/backlink-audit/competitor-analysis)

### Contextual Link

A link placed within the main body content of a page, surrounded by relevant text. More valuable than sidebar, footer, or comment links.

[Full guide: Contextual Links →](/glossary/contextual-link)

### Co-Occurrence

When specific terms frequently appear near a link. Search engines use surrounding text context to better understand link relationships.

---

## D

### Deep Link

A backlink pointing to an internal page rather than the homepage. Deep links are valuable for distributing authority throughout a site.

### Disavow

The process of telling Google to ignore certain backlinks when assessing your site. Used to address toxic or spammy links that could trigger penalties.

[Full guide: Disavow Guide →](/backlink-audit/disavow-guide)

### Do-Follow Link

A standard link that passes link equity (PageRank) to the target page. The default link type unless rel="nofollow" or similar attributes are added.

[Full guide: Do-Follow Links →](/glossary/dofollow-link)

### Domain Authority (DA)

A Moz metric (0-100) predicting how likely a domain is to rank in search results. Based on linking root domains and total links. Not a Google metric.

[Full guide: Domain Authority →](/glossary/domain-authority)

### Domain Rating (DR)

An Ahrefs metric (0-100) measuring the strength of a website's backlink profile. Based on referring domains and their authority.

---

## E

### Editorial Link

A link given naturally by another site because they genuinely want to reference your content. The most valuable type of backlink—earned, not requested.

[Full guide: Editorial Links →](/glossary/editorial-link)

### Exact Match Anchor Text

Anchor text that exactly matches the target keyword you want to rank for. Overuse can appear manipulative; natural profiles use variety.

### External Link

A link from one domain to a different domain. The opposite of internal links. Your backlinks are external links from other sites.

---

## F

### Footer Link

A link placed in a website's footer that appears on every page. Site-wide footer links often carry less weight and can appear spammy if unnatural.

### Follow Link

See: Do-Follow Link. A link that passes ranking signals.

---

## G

### Google Penalty

A ranking demotion or deindexing resulting from violating Google's guidelines. Manual penalties require a reconsideration request; algorithmic penalties lift when issues are fixed.

### Google Search Console

Free Google tool showing your site's search performance, indexing status, and backlink data. Essential for monitoring link-related issues.

### Grey Hat SEO

Techniques that fall between white hat and black hat—not explicitly banned but risky. Examples include aggressive link exchanges or borderline paid placements.

### Guest Blogging/Guest Posting

Writing content for other websites to earn backlinks. Still effective when done for genuine audiences on relevant, quality sites.

[Tactic: Guest Posting →](/link-building-tactics/guest-posting)

---

## H

### HARO (Help A Reporter Out)

A service connecting journalists with expert sources. Responding to queries can earn high-authority media links. Now part of Connectively.

[Tactic: HARO Alternatives →](/link-building-tactics/haro-alternatives)

### Homepage Link

A backlink pointing to a website's homepage. Common but can miss opportunity to build deep page authority. Best combined with deep links.

---

## I

### Inbound Link

Synonymous with backlink—a link coming into your site from an external source.

### Internal Link

A link from one page to another page on the same domain. Important for site architecture and distributing link equity.

### IP Diversity

Having backlinks from websites hosted on different IP addresses. Diverse IPs suggest natural link building; same-IP links may indicate manipulation.

---

## L

### Link Bait

Content specifically designed to attract links through its inherent shareability, controversy, or usefulness. Examples: original research, tools, comprehensive guides.

### Link Building

The process of acquiring hyperlinks from other websites to your own. A fundamental SEO practice for improving search rankings.

[Hub: Link Building →](/link-building)

### Link Burst

A sudden spike in new backlinks to a page. Natural for viral content or news; suspicious for older pages and may trigger scrutiny.

### Link Equity

The ranking value passed from one page to another through links. Formerly called "link juice." Do-follow links pass equity; nofollow typically doesn't.

[Full guide: Link Equity →](/glossary/link-equity)

### Link Exchange

A reciprocal arrangement where two sites agree to link to each other. Limited exchanges are natural; large-scale link schemes violate guidelines.

### Link Farm

A group of websites that all interlink for the purpose of manipulating search rankings. A black hat technique that results in penalties.

[Full guide: Link Farms →](/glossary/link-farm)

### Link Gap Analysis

Identifying domains that link to competitors but not to you. A core prospecting technique for finding link opportunities.

### Link Graph

The network of links connecting websites across the internet. Search engines analyse this graph to determine relationships and authority.

### Link Juice

Colloquial term for link equity—the ranking power passed through links.

### Link Network

A collection of sites under common ownership or control that interlink to manipulate rankings. Private Blog Networks (PBNs) are an example.

### Link Placement

Where a link appears within a page. Contextual body links typically carry more weight than sidebar, footer, or comment links.

### Link Profile

The complete picture of all backlinks pointing to a website, including their sources, anchor text distribution, and quality mix.

[Hub: Backlink Profile →](/glossary/link-profile)

### Link Prospect

A potential website that could provide a backlink. Good prospects are relevant, authoritative, and likely to link.

### Link Reclamation

Finding and fixing lost links—either broken links to your site or unlinked brand mentions.

[Tactic: Link Reclamation →](/link-building-tactics/link-reclamation)

### Link Rot

The gradual loss of backlinks over time as pages are removed, URLs change, or sites shut down. Requires ongoing link building to offset.

### Link Scheme

Any pattern of links intended to manipulate rankings, including buying links, excessive exchanges, or automated link building. Against Google's guidelines.

### Link Spam

Low-quality links created to manipulate rankings. Includes comment spam, forum spam, and links from irrelevant sites.

### Link Velocity

The rate at which a site gains (or loses) backlinks over time. Natural velocity varies; sudden spikes can appear suspicious.

[Full guide: Link Velocity →](/glossary/link-velocity)

### Linkable Asset

Content specifically created to attract backlinks. Examples: original research, tools, definitive guides, infographics.

[Full guide: Linkable Assets →](/link-building/linkable-assets)

### Linked Unstructured Citation

A business mention that includes a link, typically in directories or news mentions.

---

## M

### Majestic

A link analysis tool providing metrics like Trust Flow and Citation Flow. Known for its extensive link database.

### Manual Action

A penalty manually applied by a Google reviewer (vs algorithmic). Shown in Google Search Console with specific guidance for resolution.

### Media Links

Backlinks from news outlets, magazines, and journalists. High-authority and valuable; typically earned through digital PR.

### Mention

A reference to your brand or content without a hyperlink. Unlinked mentions are link building opportunities.

### Money Page

A page designed to convert visitors (product pages, service pages). Link building often targets these or pages that link to them.

### Moz

An SEO software company that developed Domain Authority (DA) and Page Authority (PA) metrics.

---

## N

### Natural Link

A backlink given editorially without request or compensation. The ideal type of link—earned through quality content.

### Negative SEO

Attempting to harm a competitor's rankings through manipulative techniques, including building spammy links to their site. Against guidelines and ethics.

### Newsjacking

Rapidly creating content about breaking news to earn media coverage and links. Requires speed and relevance.

[Tactic: Newsjacking →](/digital-pr/newsjacking)

### Nofollow Link

A link with rel="nofollow" attribute, originally telling search engines not to pass ranking signals. Google now treats as a "hint."

[Full guide: Nofollow Links →](/glossary/nofollow-link)

### Noopener/Noreferrer

Link attributes for security (noopener) and hiding referral source (noreferrer). Don't affect SEO but appear in link code.

---

## O

### Organic Link

A link given naturally without direct effort from the receiving site. The result of great content being discovered and referenced.

### Outbound Link

A link from your site to another domain. The opposite of a backlink. Quality outbound links can benefit your content.

### Outreach

The process of contacting website owners, editors, or journalists to request links or coverage.

[Hub: Outreach →](/outreach)

---

## P

### Page Authority (PA)

A Moz metric (0-100) predicting how likely a specific page is to rank. Based on links to that particular URL.

[Full guide: Page Authority →](/glossary/page-authority)

### PageRank

Google's original algorithm for measuring page importance based on links. Still exists internally though no longer publicly visible.

[Full guide: PageRank →](/glossary/pagerank)

### Partial Match Anchor

Anchor text that includes the target keyword plus other words. More natural than exact match anchors.

### PBN (Private Blog Network)

A network of sites owned by one entity, used to build links to a money site. Against Google's guidelines and risky.

[Full guide: PBN →](/glossary/pbn)

### Penguin

Google algorithm update (now part of core algorithm) targeting link spam and unnatural link patterns. First launched 2012.

### Pillow Link

A branded or generic anchor text link that helps diversify and "soften" an anchor text profile.

### Prospecting

Finding and qualifying potential link sources before outreach.

[Full guide: Prospecting →](/outreach/prospecting)

---

## R

### Reciprocal Link

A mutual link exchange between two sites. Some natural reciprocity is fine; large-scale exchanges are problematic.

### Redirect

Sending users and search engines from one URL to another. 301 redirects pass most link equity; 302s historically didn't.

### Referral Traffic

Visitors who come to your site by clicking a link on another site. A direct benefit of backlinks beyond SEO.

### Referring Domain

A unique domain that links to your website. More referring domains generally indicates a stronger backlink profile than many links from few domains.

[Full guide: Referring Domains →](/glossary/referring-domain)

### Rel Attribute

HTML attribute defining the relationship between linked pages. Common values: nofollow, sponsored, ugc, noopener.

### Relevance

How topically related a linking site is to yours. Relevant links carry more weight than links from unrelated sites.

[Full guide: Relevance Factors →](/backlink-quality/relevance-factors)

### Resource Page

A page specifically created to list helpful resources on a topic. Common link building target.

[Tactic: Resource Page Links →](/link-building-tactics/resource-page-links)

### Root Domain

The main domain without any subdomain (example.com vs blog.example.com). Referring root domains is a common metric.

---

## S

### Scholarship Link Building

Creating scholarships to earn .edu links. Once popular, now saturated and often seen as manipulative.

### Semrush

An SEO software suite with backlink analysis tools and competitive research capabilities.

### SERP (Search Engine Results Page)

The page displayed by search engines in response to a query. Backlinks influence where pages appear in SERPs.

### Sidebar Link

A link in a page's sidebar, often appearing site-wide. Generally less valuable than contextual body links.

### Site-Wide Link

A link that appears on every page of a website (navigation, footer, sidebar). One site-wide link typically counts as one link, not thousands.

### Skyscraper Technique

Finding popular linked content, creating something better, and reaching out to those linking to the original.

[Tactic: Skyscraper Technique →](/link-building-tactics/skyscraper-technique)

### Spam Score

A Moz metric indicating likelihood a site will be penalised. Based on signals common to penalised sites.

### Sponsored Attribute

rel="sponsored" marks paid or advertisement links. Introduced by Google in 2019 alongside "ugc."

[Full guide: Sponsored Links →](/glossary/sponsored-link)

---

## T

### Text Link

A standard hyperlink using text as the clickable element. More valuable than image links due to anchor text context.

### Tier 1 Links

Links pointing directly to your website. In tiered link building, these are the primary links you care about.

### Tier 2 Links

Links pointing to pages that link to you (links to your link sources). A grey hat technique to boost the power of existing backlinks.

### Topical Relevance

The degree to which a linking site or page relates to your content's subject matter. Important quality factor.

### Toxic Link

A backlink that could harm your rankings due to its spammy, manipulative, or low-quality nature.

[Full guide: Toxic Links →](/backlink-audit/toxic-links)

### Trust Flow (TF)

A Majestic metric measuring link quality based on the trustworthiness of linking sites. Higher TF indicates quality link sources.

[Full guide: Trust Flow →](/glossary/trust-flow)

---

## U

### UGC Attribute

rel="ugc" marks links within user-generated content (comments, forum posts). Introduced by Google in 2019.

[Full guide: UGC Links →](/glossary/ugc-link)

### Unlinked Mention

A reference to your brand or content that doesn't include a hyperlink. Converting these to links is a common tactic.

[Tactic: Unlinked Mentions →](/link-building-tactics/unlinked-mentions)

### URL Rating (UR)

An Ahrefs metric measuring the link strength of a specific URL (vs the domain). 0-100 scale.

---

## W

### Webmaster Guidelines

Google's published rules for websites. Violating link-related guidelines can result in penalties.

### White Hat SEO

Ethical SEO practices that follow search engine guidelines. Focuses on earning links through quality content and legitimate outreach.

[Full guide: White Hat Link Building →](/link-building/white-hat)

---

## X-Z

### XML Sitemap

A file listing pages you want search engines to index. Not directly related to backlinks but important for overall SEO.

### Zero-Click Result

A SERP result that answers the query directly, requiring no click. Affects how links drive traffic.
