## Quick Decision Guides

### Choosing an SEO Tool

**Pick Ahrefs if:**
- Link analysis is your primary need
- You want the most intuitive interface
- Content explorer features matter
- Budget allows $99+/month

**Pick Semrush if:**
- You need all-in-one SEO capabilities
- PPC data is also valuable
- You want the largest keyword database
- Technical SEO features matter

**Pick Moz if:**
- You're new to SEO
- Domain Authority is important to stakeholders
- You want included community resources
- Budget is tight for premium tools

**Pick Majestic if:**
- Deep link analysis is the focus
- You need Trust Flow/Citation Flow
- Budget is very limited
- Historical data matters most

### Choosing an Outreach Tool

**Pick Pitchbox if:**
- You run high-volume campaigns
- Automation is critical
- Budget allows $500+/month
- You have a dedicated team

**Pick BuzzStream if:**
- Relationship tracking matters most
- You want CRM functionality
- Budget is under $200/month
- Manual personalisation is preferred

### Choosing Between Methods

**Go with guest posting if:**
- You have content creation resources
- Building thought leadership matters
- You can wait 2-4 weeks per link
- You want branded placements

**Go with niche edits if:**
- You need links quickly
- Content creation is limited
- You have budget for premium placements
- You're comfortable with higher costs

**Go with in-house if:**
- Link building is core to your strategy
- You can hire and train effectively
- Long-term cost savings matter
- Control is important

**Go with agencies if:**
- You need to start immediately
- Specialised expertise is required
- Management bandwidth is limited
- You prefer variable costs
