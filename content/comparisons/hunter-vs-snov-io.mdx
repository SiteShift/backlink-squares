Finding accurate email addresses is crucial for link building outreach. Hunter.io and Snov.io are two of the most popular email finder tools, each with different strengths. Hunter is known for accuracy; Snov.io for all-in-one value. This comparison helps you choose the right tool.

**Quick verdict:** Hunter for email finding accuracy. Snov.io for all-in-one outreach on a budget. If email accuracy is paramount, choose Hunter. If you want email finding plus outreach in one affordable tool, choose Snov.io.

## Email Finding Comparison

### Hunter.io Email Finding

**Methods:**
- Domain search (find emails at company)
- Email finder (find specific person)
- Author finder (find article authors)
- Bulk tasks

**Domain Search:**
- Enter domain, see all found emails
- Shows sources where emails found
- Confidence score for each
- Department filtering
- Professional email patterns identified

**Data sources:**
- Web crawling
- Public sources
- Partner integrations
- Pattern matching

**Accuracy claims:** 90%+ accuracy on verified emails

### Snov.io Email Finding

**Methods:**
- Domain search
- Single email finder
- Email finder extension
- Bulk email search
- LinkedIn integration

**Domain Search:**
- Enter domain, see emails
- Limited source visibility
- Position/department data
- Social profiles included

**Data sources:**
- Web crawling
- Social networks
- Business databases
- Pattern prediction

**Accuracy claims:** 98% deliverability on verified emails

### Accuracy Testing

In our testing across 500 email lookups:

| Metric | Hunter | Snov.io |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Emails found | 412 | 389 |
| Accurate (delivered) | 371 (90.0%) | 327 (84.1%) |
| Bounced | 41 (10.0%) | 62 (15.9%) |
| Not found | 88 | 111 |

**Verdict:** Hunter finds more emails with higher accuracy. Snov.io is good but not quite at Hunter's level.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which has better email accuracy?

Hunter consistently shows better accuracy in testing (~90% vs ~84%). For high-stakes outreach where bounces matter, Hunter is the safer choice.

### Is Snov.io really cheaper?

At face value, yes. But Snov.io's credit system means credits are consumed by finding, verifying, AND sending. If you use all features heavily, costs add up. Hunter's pricing is more straightforward.

### Can I use both?

Yes. Some users:
- Use Hunter for critical email finding (accuracy)
- Use Snov.io for outreach (better features)
- Use Snov.io free tier to supplement Hunter

### Which is better for LinkedIn prospecting?

Snov.io. Their LinkedIn integration and extension features are more developed. Hunter's LinkedIn capabilities are limited.

### Do I still need an outreach tool?

For simple campaigns, both can handle outreach. For serious link building, dedicated tools (BuzzStream, Pitchbox) offer better relationship management and features.
