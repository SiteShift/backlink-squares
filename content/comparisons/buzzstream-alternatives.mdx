## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Pitchbox worth 20x the price of BuzzStream?

For high-volume campaigns, yes. Pitchbox's automation saves significant time at scale. If you're sending thousands of outreach emails monthly, the efficiency gains justify the cost. For smaller operations, BuzzStream offers better value.

### Do I need a separate email finder?

BuzzStream requires integrations for email finding. Many alternatives (Hunter, Snov.io, Pitchbox) include email finding. Consider your workflow when choosing.

### Which tool has the best deliverability?

Lemlist focuses heavily on deliverability with warm-up features and best practices built-in. Hunter.io's verification also helps. BuzzStream leaves deliverability largely to your email provider.

### Can I switch from BuzzStream easily?

Most alternatives allow data import. Pitchbox and Respona have BuzzStream import features. You may lose some relationship history in the transition.

### Which is best for small teams?

For small teams:
1. BuzzStream (affordable, good features)
2. Hunter (simple, effective)
3. Snov.io (good value all-in-one)
