## When to Keep Pitchbox

Despite the alternatives, Pitchbox may still be the right choice if:

### High volume operations
At 100+ emails per day, Pitchbox's automation saves significant time. The cost becomes justified by efficiency gains.

### Agency with multiple clients
Pitchbox handles multiple campaigns elegantly. Managing clients across cheaper tools gets complicated.

### Sophisticated workflow needs
If you need complex automation triggers, detailed reporting, and enterprise features, Pitchbox delivers.

### Team already trained
Switching costs matter. If your team is efficient with Pitchbox, the savings from alternatives may not offset retraining.

### ROI calculation
If Pitchbox helps you land even one extra client or significant link per month, it likely pays for itself.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can cheaper tools really replace Pitchbox?

For many teams, yes. If you're not running high-volume campaigns or don't need sophisticated automation, alternatives provide adequate features at much lower cost. You may need to combine tools or accept more manual work.

### What's the best Pitchbox alternative for agencies?

BuzzStream or Respona for most agencies. If you have enterprise clients, the value of Pitchbox may justify keeping it for those accounts while using cheaper tools for smaller clients.

### How do I migrate from Pitchbox?

Most alternatives support data import. Export your contacts and campaign data from Pitchbox, then import to your new tool. Expect some cleanup and relationship history may not transfer fully.

### Should I use multiple tools instead?

Often yes. Combining a strong email finder (Hunter) with a good CRM (BuzzStream) can provide Pitchbox-level capabilities at 87% less cost. Trade-off is managing multiple subscriptions.
