## What to Look for in a Link Building Agency

Before reviewing specific agencies, understand what separates quality providers from risky ones.

### Green Flags: Signs of a Quality Agency

**Transparent Methods**
Good agencies explain exactly how they'll build links. They discuss outreach, content creation, and relationship building—not secret techniques.

**Quality Metrics**
They target relevant sites with real traffic, not just high DA numbers on PBNs or link farms.

**Realistic Timelines**
Quality links take time. Be wary of agencies promising dozens of links in weeks.

**Case Studies and References**
Legitimate agencies have verifiable results and clients willing to speak about their experience.

**Clear Reporting**
You should see exactly which links were built, from which sites, with full URLs—not vague summaries.

**White-Hat Focus**
They follow Google's guidelines, focusing on editorial links rather than purchased placements on low-quality sites.

### Red Flags: Warning Signs

**Guaranteed Rankings**
No agency can guarantee rankings. This claim indicates either dishonesty or risky tactics.

**Impossibly Low Prices**
Quality outreach and content cost money. $50 for a DA 50 link likely means PBNs or compromised sites.

**Vague About Sources**
Refusing to explain where links come from suggests they'd embarrass the agency if revealed.

**Link Volume Focus**
Emphasizing quantity over quality indicates mass link schemes rather than strategic building.

**No Examples of Placements**
Legitimate agencies can show examples of past placements on real sites.

**Anchor Text Promises**
Guaranteeing specific anchor text suggests manipulative practices rather than natural editorial placement.

## How to Evaluate and Choose an Agency

### Step 1: Define Your Goals

Before contacting agencies:
- What link volume do you need?
- What authority levels matter?
- What's your budget range?
- What's your timeline?
- Do you need additional services?

### Step 2: Request Proposals

Contact 3-5 agencies that match your needs. Ask for:
- Detailed methodology explanation
- Sample placements or case studies
- Client references
- Specific pricing for your needs
- Timeline expectations

### Step 3: Evaluate Proposals

Compare agencies on:
- Transparency of methods
- Quality of example placements
- Pricing and value
- Communication quality
- Reference feedback

### Step 4: Start with Trial

If possible, start with a smaller engagement:
- Single campaign or project
- 3-month trial period
- Clear success metrics
- Exit clause if needed

### Step 5: Monitor Results

Track agency performance:
- Links delivered vs. promised
- Quality of placements
- Traffic from referrals
- Ranking improvements
- Communication quality

## Questions to Ask Link Building Agencies

**About Methods**
- How exactly will you build links for us?
- Can you show examples of past placements?
- Do you use any owned properties or networks?
- How do you handle anchor text?

**About Quality**
- How do you vet potential link sources?
- What metrics determine a quality placement?
- How do you ensure relevance?
- What's your rejection rate on potential placements?

**About Process**
- What information do you need from us?
- How will you report progress?
- What's your typical timeline for results?
- Who will be our main contact?

**About Risk**
- Have any clients experienced penalties?
- How do you stay within Google guidelines?
- What happens if a link is removed?
- Do you guarantee placements?

## FAQ

### How much should I budget for a link building agency?

Expect $500-2,000 per quality link from reputable agencies, or $3,000-10,000+ monthly for retainer services. Budget $100-300 per link for more affordable productized services, understanding quality may vary.

### Can link building agencies guarantee results?

No legitimate agency guarantees specific rankings. They can guarantee deliverables (number of links, domain authority targets) but not ranking outcomes. Be wary of ranking guarantees—they suggest risky tactics.

### How long does it take to see results from agency link building?

Expect 3-6 months before seeing significant ranking impact from new links. Initial link placements typically happen within 4-8 weeks of campaign start, but SEO impact takes longer to materialize.

### Should I use an agency or build links in-house?

Agencies make sense when you lack expertise, time, or relationships. In-house works better when you have resources and want direct control. Many companies use hybrid approaches—in-house strategy with agency execution.

### How do I verify an agency is building quality links?

Request specific placement URLs and verify: Is the site real with actual traffic? Does the content make sense? Would you be comfortable if Google reviewed these links? Check placements in Ahrefs or Moz for metrics.
