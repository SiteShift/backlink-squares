# Building Links Through Startup Communities: Networks, Accelerators, and Ecosystems

Startup communities offer a unique link building advantage: they're specifically designed to support and promote member companies. Accelerators, incubators, startup networks, and entrepreneurial organizations actively link to portfolio and member companies.

For startups, community link building provides relevant, authoritative links while building valuable business relationships.

## Why Community Links Work for Startups

Community links offer specific benefits:

**Built-in promotion:** These organizations exist to support you—linking is natural.

**High relevance:** Links from startup-focused sites send appropriate signals.

**Network effects:** Community involvement leads to introductions and opportunities.

**Ongoing value:** Membership often means ongoing link maintenance and updates.

**Multiple touchpoints:** Single memberships yield multiple link opportunities.

## Types of Startup Community Link Sources

### Accelerators and Incubators

Accelerator programs actively promote portfolio companies:

**Link opportunities:**
- Portfolio company pages
- Batch/cohort announcements
- Demo day coverage
- Alumni directories
- Success story features

**Top accelerators (high DA):**
- Y Combinator (yc.co, ycombinator.com)
- Techstars (techstars.com)
- 500 Startups (500.co)
- Plug and Play (plugandplaytechcenter.com)
- Entrepreneur First (joinef.com)

**Vertical accelerators:**
- Healthcare: Rock Health, Blueprint Health, Dreamit Health
- Fintech: Barclays Accelerator, Finovate
- EdTech: Imagine K12, LearnLaunch
- Climate: Elemental Excelerator, Greentown Labs

## Getting Into Accelerators for Links (and More)

### Application Strategy

Accelerator selection should consider link value alongside program quality:

**High-value factors:**
- Domain authority of accelerator website
- Quality of portfolio page
- Ongoing alumni engagement
- Media coverage of program

**Application tips:**
- Apply to programs aligned with your stage
- Demonstrate traction appropriate for program
- Show coachability and growth potential
- Reference connections to alumni if any

### Maximizing Accelerator Link Value

Once accepted:

**During program:**
- Ensure portfolio listing is complete
- Provide high-quality logo and description
- Update information as you progress
- Participate in linkable activities

**Post-program:**
- Maintain alumni connections
- Participate in alumni events
- Update success metrics for features
- Engage with newer cohorts

## Building Your Community Link Strategy

### Audit Current Memberships

Start by identifying existing opportunities:

**Questions to ask:**
- What accelerator/incubator were you part of? (check portfolio pages)
- What investors have you received funding from? (check portfolio pages)
- What organizations are you a member of? (check member directories)
- What events have you participated in? (check archives)

### Prioritize New Memberships

Evaluate new opportunities by:

**Link value:**
- Domain authority
- Link type (dofollow vs. nofollow)
- Page authority of directory page
- Traffic to listing pages

**Business value:**
- Network quality
- Program support
- Resource access
- Cost vs. benefit

### Create Maintenance System

Community links require ongoing attention:

**Monthly:**
- Verify listings are active
- Update information as needed
- Engage with community content

**Quarterly:**
- Review membership value
- Identify new opportunities
- Pursue feature opportunities

**Annually:**
- Comprehensive directory audit
- Membership evaluation
- Community strategy review

## Measuring Community Link Success

### Link Metrics

- **Community referring domains:** Total links from community sources
- **Domain authority:** Average DA of community sites
- **Link diversity:** Mix of accelerator, network, event links
- **Geographic coverage:** Local, regional, national sources

### Engagement Metrics

- **Community involvement:** Events attended, content contributed
- **Network growth:** Connections made through communities
- **Opportunity flow:** Introductions and leads from communities

### Benchmarks

| Source Type | Links Possible | Typical DA |
|-------------|----------------|------------|
| Top accelerators | 2-5 per program | 60-80+ |
| Startup networks | 1-3 per membership | 40-60 |
| Events/competitions | 1-2 per event | 30-60 |
| University programs | 1-3 per program | 70-90+ |
| Regional organizations | 1-2 per membership | 30-50 |

## Common Community Link Mistakes

**Ignoring accelerator alumni status:** Alumni pages often remain active for years—verify yours.

**Incomplete profiles:** Missing information means weaker listings.

**One-and-done approach:** Community link building requires ongoing maintenance.

**Undervaluing local organizations:** Regional links provide important geographic signals.

**Skipping less prominent programs:** Smaller accelerators often provide more attention and support.
