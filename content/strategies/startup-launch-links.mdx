Your product launch represents a unique, time-limited link building opportunity. For a brief window, you have something genuinely newsworthy—a story journalists might actually want to cover. The startups that maximize this window can secure 20-50+ high-authority links in days.

The ones that waste it? They struggle to earn those same links over months.

This guide covers how to extract maximum link value from your launch.

## Why Launch Windows Matter for Link Building

Product launches are newsworthy by definition. Unlike ongoing "we're doing interesting things" pitches, launches offer:

**Timeliness:** Journalists have a reason to cover you *now*
**Novelty:** You're introducing something new
**Exclusivity opportunities:** You can offer early access and exclusive angles
**Concentrated attention:** All your promotional efforts align

The challenge: this window closes quickly. Within 1-2 weeks, your "launch" becomes old news.

## The Launch Link Building Timeline

### 8-12 Weeks Before Launch: Relationship Building

Don't pitch yet—build relationships:

**Identify targets:**
- Journalists covering your space
- Bloggers writing for your audience
- Industry influencers who might cover launches
- Podcasters who feature new products

**Build connections:**
- Follow on social media
- Engage with their content genuinely
- Share their work with your audience
- Comment thoughtfully on their articles

**Research what they cover:**
- Note their past launch coverage
- Understand their audience
- Identify what angles interest them

### 4-6 Weeks Before Launch: Prepare Assets

Create everything you'll need:

**Press kit:**
- Company overview (1 page)
- Founder bios and headshots
- Product description and screenshots
- Key metrics and traction data
- FAQ document
- High-resolution logos and images

**Content assets:**
- Blog post announcing launch
- Landing page for launch
- Demo video or product tour
- Data or research that supports your story

**Outreach materials:**
- Media list with contact details
- Pitch templates (multiple angles)
- Exclusive offer details
- Embargo arrangements if applicable

### 2-4 Weeks Before Launch: Begin Outreach

Start contacting journalists and bloggers:

**Exclusive pitches (top tier):**
- Offer 1-2 top publications exclusive early access
- Provide embargo dates for coordinated coverage
- Be clear about exclusivity terms

**Early access pitches (second tier):**
- Offer product access before launch
- No exclusivity, but early review opportunity
- Request coverage timing around launch

**General pitches (broader outreach):**
- Begin sending pitches to wider list
- Follow up methodically
- Track responses and adjust angles

### Launch Week: Execute and Maximize

**Launch day:**
- Publish your announcement content
- Notify all confirmed coverage contacts
- Execute social media promotion
- Submit to relevant communities (Product Hunt, Hacker News, etc.)
- Monitor for coverage and engage

**Days 2-7:**
- Follow up with pending pitches
- Send new pitches to outlets that didn't respond
- Reach out to anyone who showed interest
- Create reactive content based on coverage

**Days 8-14:**
- Conclude active outreach
- Thank journalists who covered you
- Document results and learnings
- Transition to ongoing link building

## Pitch Angles for Maximum Coverage

### The Innovation Angle

Focus on what's genuinely new:

```
Subject: [Specific innovation] launching [date] - [Company Name]

Hi [Name],

On [date], we're launching [Product]—the first [category] that [specific innovation].

Unlike [existing solutions] that [limitation], [Product] [what makes it different].

We've been testing with [notable beta users/companies] and seen [specific result].

I'd love to offer you early access before our public launch. Would that be interesting?

[Your name]
```

### The Data/Trend Angle

Connect to larger trends:

```
Subject: [Trend] data + new product launch

Hi [Name],

I noticed your recent piece on [trend]. Thought you might be interested in related data we've collected:

[Key data point 1]
[Key data point 2]

This informed our decision to build [Product], which we're launching [date]. It's designed specifically to address [problem illuminated by data].

Would you be interested in either the data for a story, or covering the launch?

[Your name]
```

### The Founder Story Angle

Humanize the launch:

```
Subject: From [interesting background] to launching [Product]

Hi [Name],

After [founder's relevant experience/story], I'm launching [Product] on [date].

[1-2 sentences on how experience led to building this]

The product [brief description and differentiator].

Would you be interested in the story behind why we built this?

[Your name]
```

## Platform-Specific Launch Strategies

### Product Hunt

Product Hunt can generate significant traffic and links:

**Preparation:**
- Build hunter relationship 2+ months ahead
- Gather early supporters for launch day
- Prepare all Product Hunt assets (images, video, tagline)
- Schedule launch for Tuesday-Thursday

**Launch day:**
- Coordinate supporter engagement
- Respond to every comment
- Share on all your channels
- Engage throughout the day

**Link value:**
- Product Hunt profile link
- Coverage from Product Hunt-watching publications
- Social proof for other pitches

### Hacker News

Technical products can gain traction on HN:

**Best practices:**
- Post during US morning hours
- Title: straightforward, not clickbait
- Have founder available to answer questions
- "Show HN" format for genuine product launches

**Link value:**
- HN post itself (nofollow but high traffic)
- Coverage from HN-watching tech journalists
- Developer community awareness

### Industry-Specific Platforms

Identify relevant communities:

- Beta testing platforms (BetaList, BetaPage)
- Industry-specific forums and communities
- Subreddits (follow rules carefully)
- Slack groups and Discord servers
- Newsletter submissions

## Building on Launch Success

### Converting Coverage to Ongoing Relationships

Journalists who cover your launch are warm contacts:

- Thank them genuinely (no ask attached)
- Stay in touch periodically
- Offer to be a source for future stories
- Share relevant updates (sparingly)

### Creating Follow-Up Content

Launch coverage creates opportunities:

- "What we learned from launching to 10,000 users"
- Follow-up data and metrics posts
- Response to feedback received
- Feature announcements building on launch

### Transitioning to Sustainable Link Building

Post-launch, shift to ongoing tactics:

- Guest posting to publications that covered you
- Content marketing building on launch themes
- Partnership development with launch supporters
- Community building in channels that drove traffic

## Measuring Launch Link Building Success

### Link Metrics

- **Total referring domains:** New domains linking post-launch
- **High-authority links:** DR 50+ publications linking
- **Link velocity:** Links acquired per day during launch window
- **Link types:** Editorial vs. directory/forum links

### Coverage Metrics

- **Total coverage pieces:** Articles mentioning your launch
- **Coverage tier:** Distribution across publication tiers
- **Message penetration:** Key messages in coverage
- **Exclusive performance:** ROI on exclusive arrangements

### Benchmarks by Company Stage

| Stage | Links (Launch Window) | Coverage Pieces |
|-------|----------------------|-----------------|
| Pre-seed, unknown founder | 5-15 | 3-10 |
| Seed, some traction | 15-30 | 10-25 |
| Series A, proven product | 30-60 | 25-50+ |
| Notable founder/team | 50-100+ | 50-100+ |

## Common Launch Link Building Mistakes

**Starting too late:** Relationship building takes months. Starting 2 weeks before launch is too late.

**Wasting exclusives:** Exclusive access to a mid-tier publication wastes your best asset.

**One-angle pitching:** Different journalists need different angles. Customize.

**Neglecting communities:** Product Hunt, HN, and niche communities can outperform traditional PR.

**No follow-up:** Most coverage comes from follow-up, not initial pitch.

**Post-launch disappearance:** The journalists who covered you are your best contacts. Don't vanish.
