Journalist query services connect experts with journalists seeking sources. By responding effectively, you can earn high-authority links from major publications. This guide covers HARO and its alternatives.

## How Journalist Query Services Work

### The Basic Model

1. **Journalists** post queries seeking expert sources
2. **Experts** receive queries and respond
3. **Journalists** select useful responses
4. **Selected experts** get quoted with links

### Why This Works for Links

**High-quality opportunities**: Major publications use these services
**Editorial context**: Natural, contextual mentions
**Authority signal**: Being cited as expert
**Passive-ish**: Queries come to you

### The Reality Check

**Competition**: Many responses per query
**Hit rate**: Most responses don't get selected
**Time investment**: Requires regular monitoring
**Quality matters**: Generic responses fail

## HARO (Help a Reporter Out)

### Overview

The original and best-known platform.

**How it works**:
- Sign up as source
- Receive 3 emails daily with queries
- Respond to relevant queries
- Wait for selection

**Query categories**:
- Business and Finance
- High Tech
- Lifestyle and Fitness
- Entertainment and Media
- Travel
- Education
- Healthcare
- General

### Pros and Cons

**Pros**:
- Large journalist base
- Major publications use it
- Free basic tier
- Established platform

**Cons**:
- Very competitive
- Many low-quality queries
- Time-consuming to monitor
- Free tier has limitations

### Pricing

- **Basic**: Free (3 emails daily)
- **Standard**: $19/month (keyword alerts)
- **Advanced**: $49/month (profile features)
- **Premium**: $149/month (early access, more features)

## HARO Alternatives

### Featured (formerly Terkel)

**Focus**: Quality over quantity

**How it works**:
- Curated queries
- Expert profiles
- Match-based distribution
- Response scoring

**Pros**:
- Higher-quality queries
- Better matching
- Less spam
- Profile building

**Cons**:
- Smaller query volume
- Newer platform
- Paid tiers for full access

### Quoted (by BuzzStream)

**Focus**: SEO-aware journalist connections

**How it works**:
- Query distribution
- SEO-focused features
- Link tracking
- Response templates

**Pros**:
- Built for link builders
- Good categorization
- Response tracking

**Cons**:
- Newer, smaller network
- Less established

### Qwoted

**Focus**: Verified journalists only

**How it works**:
- Journalist verification
- Expert profiles
- Query matching
- Ongoing relationships

**Pros**:
- Verified journalists
- Relationship focus
- Quality queries

**Cons**:
- Smaller network
- Requires profile investment

### SourceBottle

**Focus**: Multi-market

**How it works**:
- Query distribution
- Multiple countries
- Category filtering

**Pros**:
- Good for non-US markets
- Free tier available
- Regular queries

**Cons**:
- Smaller than HARO
- Variable quality

### Direct Journalist Outreach

**Alternative approach**: Build relationships directly

**Methods**:
- Twitter/X journalist connections
- LinkedIn networking
- Direct email relationships
- Conference connections

## Responding Effectively

### What Journalists Want

**Quick**: Fast responses preferred
**Relevant**: Actually answer the question
**Quotable**: Provide usable quotes
**Credible**: Clear expertise demonstrated
**Unique**: Different perspective from others

### Response Structure

**Essential elements**:

1. **Credentials**: Why you're qualified (brief)
2. **Direct answer**: Respond to the actual question
3. **Quotable content**: Ready-to-use statements
4. **Supporting detail**: Context if needed
5. **Contact info**: Email, bio link

**Response template**:

```
Hi [Journalist name],

[Your name], [Title] at [Company]. [One sentence of
relevant credentials].

[Direct response to their question - 2-3 sentences of
quotable content].

[Supporting detail or example if helpful - 1-2 sentences].

Happy to provide additional context or clarification.

[Your name]
[Title]
[Email]
[Website/LinkedIn]
```

### Response Best Practices

**Do**:
- Respond quickly (within hours)
- Answer the specific question asked
- Provide quotable sound bites
- Show relevant expertise
- Be concise (under 300 words)
- Include brief bio/credentials

**Don't**:
- Ignore the actual question
- Write essay-length responses
- Promote your business directly
- Respond to irrelevant queries
- Send generic responses
- Be pushy about links

## Maximising Success

### Query Selection

**Respond to**:
- Queries matching your expertise
- Topics you can genuinely add value to
- Publications worth your time
- Specific questions you can answer

**Skip**:
- Generic or unclear queries
- Topics outside your expertise
- Very competitive queries (unless strong angle)
- Low-quality publications

### Standing Out

**Differentiation strategies**:
- Unique perspective others won't have
- Specific data or examples
- Contrarian (but defensible) viewpoint
- Personal experience or case study
- Quick response time

### Building Relationships

**For repeat opportunities**:
- Deliver excellent responses
- Follow up after publication
- Connect on social media
- Share their articles
- Be available for follow-ups

## Tracking Results

### What to Track

**Activity metrics**:
- Queries received
- Responses sent
- Response rate

**Success metrics**:
- Responses selected
- Articles published
- Links earned
- Publication quality

### ROI Assessment

Calculate value:

```
Hours spent × Hourly value = Time cost
Links earned × Average link value = Link value
Link value - Time cost = Net value
```

**Typical benchmarks**:
- 5-20 responses per success
- 1-5 hours per link earned
- Variable link quality

## Common Mistakes

### Wrong Query Selection

**Mistake**: Responding to everything

**Problem**: Wastes time on poor matches

**Solution**: Be selective; respond only when strong fit

### Generic Responses

**Mistake**: Same response regardless of query

**Problem**: Doesn't stand out; rarely selected

**Solution**: Customise each response to specific query

### Missing the Point

**Mistake**: Not actually answering the question

**Problem**: Journalist can't use response

**Solution**: Read carefully; answer what's asked

### Too Promotional

**Mistake**: Pitching your business instead of helping

**Problem**: Journalist wants expert, not advertisement

**Solution**: Focus on value; let links come naturally

### Slow Response

**Mistake**: Responding days after query posted

**Problem**: Journalist has already moved on

**Solution**: Respond within hours of receiving query
