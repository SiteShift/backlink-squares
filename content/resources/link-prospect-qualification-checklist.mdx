Not all link opportunities are worth pursuing. Effective link building requires systematic qualification to focus effort on prospects most likely to convert into valuable links.

This checklist provides a two-stage approach: quick filtering to eliminate poor prospects fast, and deep analysis for priority evaluation.

## Stage 1: Quick Scan Filter (60 Seconds)

Complete this section first. If any item fails, disqualify the prospect and move on.

### Instant Disqualifiers

- [ ] Site loads properly (no errors, not parked)
- [ ] Content is in target language
- [ ] Site appears legitimate (not obvious spam)
- [ ] Has some relevance to your content/industry
- [ ] Not a direct competitor's site

**If ANY checkbox is unchecked**: Skip this prospect immediately. Do not proceed to Stage 2.

### Quick Quality Indicators

Check these to determine if deeper analysis is warranted:

- [ ] Domain authority meets minimum threshold (DA ___+)
- [ ] Site has real content (not just aggregation)
- [ ] Updated within last 12 months
- [ ] Not obviously a PBN or link farm

**If 3+ items checked**: Proceed to Stage 2 for full evaluation.

**If 2 or fewer**: Low priority. Only pursue if prospect volume is limited.

### Section C: Content Quality Assessment

**Site-Level Content**

- [ ] Original, substantive content throughout
- [ ] Professional writing quality
- [ ] Regular content updates (activity within 90 days)
- [ ] Clear authorship information
- [ ] Proper citations and references where appropriate

**Page-Level Content** (specific page for link)

- [ ] Page has meaningful content (not thin)
- [ ] Content is well-organised and readable
- [ ] Provides genuine value to visitors
- [ ] Not primarily a link aggregation page
- [ ] Links are contextual, not just listed

**Content Red Flags**

Check for these warning signs:

- [ ] NOT thin content (less than 300 words with no substance)
- [ ] NOT obviously AI-generated without editing
- [ ] NOT scraped or duplicate content
- [ ] NOT keyword-stuffed
- [ ] NOT focused primarily on selling links

## Decision Matrix

### Go/No-Go Criteria

**Must Have (All required)**:
- [ ] Relevance score 3+ (out of 5)
- [ ] Not a spam site (E score 5+)
- [ ] Some path to getting link (F score 4+)

**Strong Indicators (2+ preferred)**:
- [ ] DA/DR 30+
- [ ] Real traffic (1,000+ monthly)
- [ ] Content quality score 6+
- [ ] Trust indicators present

### Final Decision

- [ ] **PURSUE**: Meets all must-haves + 2+ strong indicators
- [ ] **MAYBE**: Meets all must-haves + 1 strong indicator
- [ ] **SKIP**: Missing any must-have OR low overall score

## Common Qualification Mistakes

### Over-Qualification

- Rejecting good opportunities due to one imperfect metric
- Setting DA threshold too high for your domain
- Expecting perfect relevance match

### Under-Qualification

- Accepting any site willing to link
- Ignoring spam signals
- Not checking content quality

### Right Balance

- Focus on relevance and quality over raw metrics
- Consider your own domain's authority level
- Trust your judgment on borderline cases
