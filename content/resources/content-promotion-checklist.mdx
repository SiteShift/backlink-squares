Publishing great content is only half the battle. Without systematic promotion, even exceptional content fails to attract the links it deserves.

This checklist provides a timeline-based approach to content promotion, from pre-launch preparation through long-term link building. Follow it for every significant piece of content you publish.

## Phase 1: Pre-Launch Preparation (1-2 Weeks Before)

### Content Optimisation

- [ ] Title optimised for search and sharing
- [ ] Meta description compelling and keyword-rich
- [ ] URL is clean and descriptive
- [ ] Header tags properly structured (H1, H2, H3)
- [ ] Internal links to relevant site content
- [ ] External links to authoritative sources
- [ ] Images optimised (compressed, alt text, descriptive filenames)

### Linkable Elements

- [ ] Original data, research, or statistics included
- [ ] Quotable insights or takeaways highlighted
- [ ] Downloadable resources or tools if applicable
- [ ] Visual elements (infographics, charts, diagrams)
- [ ] Expert quotes or contributions included

### Technical Readiness

- [ ] Page loads quickly (under 3 seconds)
- [ ] Mobile display tested and working
- [ ] Social sharing buttons functional
- [ ] Open Graph tags configured for social sharing
- [ ] Twitter Card tags configured
- [ ] Schema markup implemented if applicable

### Promotion Planning

- [ ] Target promotion channels identified
- [ ] Outreach prospect list prepared (minimum 50 targets)
- [ ] Social media posts drafted
- [ ] Email newsletter copy prepared (if applicable)
- [ ] Influencer/expert notification list created

---

## Phase 2: Launch Day Activities

### Immediate Post-Publish (First 2 Hours)

- [ ] Verify content is live and loading correctly
- [ ] Verify all links working
- [ ] Verify images displaying
- [ ] Verify social sharing functioning
- [ ] Submit URL to Google Search Console for indexing

### Social Media Distribution

**LinkedIn**:
- [ ] Personal profile post with commentary
- [ ] Company page post (if applicable)
- [ ] Relevant group shares (if permitted)

**Twitter/X**:
- [ ] Tweet with compelling hook
- [ ] Thread if content warrants it
- [ ] Tag relevant accounts who might share

**Facebook**:
- [ ] Personal/business page post
- [ ] Relevant group shares (where appropriate)

**Other Platforms** (as relevant):
- [ ] Reddit (appropriate subreddits, genuine engagement)
- [ ] Hacker News (if tech-relevant)
- [ ] Industry-specific communities
- [ ] Slack/Discord communities (where permitted)

### Email Notifications

- [ ] Email newsletter sent (if scheduled)
- [ ] Personal emails to people mentioned in content
- [ ] Emails to experts quoted in content
- [ ] Notification to internal team for sharing

### Initial Outreach

- [ ] Send 10-20 high-priority outreach emails
- [ ] Focus on warmest contacts first
- [ ] Log all outreach in tracking system

---

## Phase 3: Week One Promotion

### Sustained Social Engagement

- [ ] Respond to all comments and shares
- [ ] Share user feedback and engagement
- [ ] Repurpose key points for additional posts
- [ ] Schedule reminder posts for different time zones

### Content Syndication

- [ ] Medium republish (with canonical)
- [ ] LinkedIn article version (if appropriate)
- [ ] Industry publication syndication requests
- [ ] Partner site republishing agreements

### Outreach Continuation

**Daily Outreach Targets**:
| Day | Emails to Send | Focus Area |
|-----|----------------|------------|
| Day 2 | 10-15 | Resource page owners |
| Day 3 | 10-15 | Bloggers in niche |
| Day 4 | 10-15 | Journalists/publications |
| Day 5 | 10-15 | Industry influencers |

- [ ] Track all outreach sent
- [ ] Log responses received
- [ ] Initiate follow-up sequence for non-responders

### Community Engagement

- [ ] Participate in relevant online discussions
- [ ] Answer related questions on Quora
- [ ] Engage in relevant forum threads
- [ ] Comment on related blog posts (genuinely, not for links)

### Monitor and Document

- [ ] Track initial backlinks acquired
- [ ] Document social engagement metrics
- [ ] Note high-performing promotion channels
- [ ] Identify opportunities for improvement

---

## Phase 4: Week Two-Four Promotion

### Follow-Up Outreach

- [ ] First follow-up to non-responders (Day 7-10)
- [ ] Second follow-up to non-responders (Day 14-17)
- [ ] New outreach to secondary prospect list
- [ ] Outreach to sites that linked to similar content

### Content Repurposing

Transform your content into multiple formats:

- [ ] **Infographic**: Visual summary of key points
- [ ] **SlideShare/Presentation**: Slide deck version
- [ ] **Video**: YouTube/Vimeo summary or tutorial
- [ ] **Podcast mention**: Pitch to relevant podcasts
- [ ] **Email course**: Series breaking down the content
- [ ] **Social graphics**: Quote cards, stat graphics

### PR and Media Outreach

- [ ] Identify journalists covering this topic
- [ ] Prepare press angle or hook
- [ ] Send targeted media pitches
- [ ] Offer exclusive data or interviews

### Influencer Engagement

- [ ] Identify influencers who share similar content
- [ ] Engage with their content genuinely
- [ ] Share your content with them personally
- [ ] Offer collaboration opportunities

### Guest Posting with Links

- [ ] Identify guest post opportunities
- [ ] Pitch topics that allow linking back
- [ ] Write and submit guest posts
- [ ] Follow up on pending submissions

---

## Phase 5: Ongoing Promotion (Month 2+)

### Continuous Link Building

- [ ] Add to evergreen outreach rotation
- [ ] Include in relevant guest post author bios
- [ ] Link from new content you create
- [ ] Suggest as resource for broken link replacements

### Content Refreshing

- [ ] Update statistics and data points
- [ ] Add new sections based on industry changes
- [ ] Refresh visuals and formatting
- [ ] Re-promote updated version

### Relationship Leveraging

- [ ] Follow up with people who linked
- [ ] Ask for shares or additional coverage
- [ ] Explore collaboration opportunities
- [ ] Build on relationships for future content

### Monitoring and Maintenance

- [ ] Monthly backlink check
- [ ] Monitor for broken links to/from content
- [ ] Track ranking progress for target keywords
- [ ] Document learnings for future content

## Promotion Channel Reference

### High-Value Channels

| Channel | Effort | Link Potential | Best For |
|---------|--------|----------------|----------|
| Direct outreach | High | High | All content types |
| Guest posting | High | High | Evergreen content |
| Social media | Medium | Low-Medium | Viral content |
| Email newsletter | Low | Low | Existing audience |
| PR/Media | High | High | Newsworthy content |
| Content syndication | Medium | Low | Thought leadership |

### Channel-Specific Tips

**Direct Outreach**:
- Personalise every email
- Lead with value, not ask
- Follow up systematically

**Guest Posting**:
- Pitch topics that naturally include your link
- Write high-quality posts that make editors want more
- Build ongoing relationships

**Social Media**:
- Engage in conversations, don't just broadcast
- Use native formats (threads, carousels)
- Time posts for maximum visibility

**PR/Media**:
- Newsjack relevant trends
- Provide exclusive access or data
- Build journalist relationships before you need them

## Outreach Templates Quick Reference

### Resource Page Request

```
Hi [Name],

Found your resource page on [topic] - great collection.

I recently published [brief description] that might fit:
[URL]

It covers [unique angle/value].

Worth a look for your resources?

[Your name]
```

### Influencer Share Request

```
Hi [Name],

Big fan of your work on [topic]. Your [specific piece]
really resonated.

I just published something I think your audience would
find valuable: [URL]

It's about [brief description].

Would love your thoughts if you get a chance to read it.

[Your name]
```

### Expert Quote Follow-Up

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks again for your contribution to my piece on [topic].

It's now live: [URL]

Your insight on [specific point] really elevated the article.

Feel free to share with your network if you think it would
interest them.

[Your name]
```

## Common Promotion Mistakes

### What to Avoid

- [ ] Publishing and forgetting (no promotion)
- [ ] All social, no outreach
- [ ] One-time push, no sustained effort
- [ ] Generic outreach templates
- [ ] Targeting irrelevant sites
- [ ] Giving up after initial push

### What to Do Instead

- [ ] Plan promotion before publishing
- [ ] Balance channels (social + outreach + PR)
- [ ] Promote consistently for 4+ weeks
- [ ] Personalise every outreach email
- [ ] Target relevant, high-quality sites
- [ ] Persist with follow-ups
