## How to Use These Resources

### Templates

**Customise every time**: Templates are starting points, not finished products

**Match the context**: Adapt tone, specifics, and approach to each recipient

**Test and iterate**: Track what works and refine your approach

### Checklists

**Use consistently**: Same evaluation criteria for fair comparisons

**Adapt to your needs**: Add or modify criteria for your situation

**Document decisions**: Record why you included or excluded prospects

### Calculators

**Input realistic numbers**: Results are only as good as inputs

**Use for planning**: Estimate before committing resources

**Track actual results**: Compare predictions to reality
