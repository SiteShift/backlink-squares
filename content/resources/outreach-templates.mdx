These templates provide proven structures for link building outreach. Always customise for each recipient—generic, unchanged templates underperform.

## How to Use These Templates

### Customisation Requirements

**Every email must include**:
- Recipient's actual name
- Specific reference to their content
- Why your content fits their context
- Clear, easy action

**Never send**:
- Unchanged templates
- Obvious placeholders ([SITE NAME])
- Generic flattery
- Mass identical emails

## Guest Posting Templates

### Initial Guest Post Pitch

```
Subject: Article idea: [Specific topic relevant to their audience]

Hi [Name],

[Specific reference to recent article they published—mention
something specific that shows you actually read it.]

I'd like to pitch a guest article on [topic]. I noticed you
haven't covered [specific angle] yet, and your readers would
find it valuable because [brief, relevant reason].

Here are a few topic ideas:

1. [Topic 1]: [One sentence explaining value/angle]
2. [Topic 2]: [One sentence explaining value/angle]
3. [Topic 3]: [One sentence explaining value/angle]

I'm [brief credential relevant to topic—not a life story].
Here's a sample of my writing: [link to relevant published piece]

Would any of these work for [Publication name]?

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Guest Post Follow-Up

```
Subject: Re: Article idea: [Topic]

Hi [Name],

Floating this back up in case it got buried—I know how
hectic inboxes can get.

I'd still love to contribute on [topic]. Quick reminder of
the ideas:

1. [Topic 1]
2. [Topic 2]

Happy to adjust the angle if something else would work better
for your audience.

[Your name]
```

### After Guest Post Acceptance

```
Subject: Re: [Topic]

Hi [Name],

Excellent—thanks for the opportunity!

I'll have the draft to you by [date]. Quick questions before
I start:

- Any word count preferences?
- Formatting requirements?
- Style guide I should follow?

Looking forward to contributing.

[Your name]
```

## Broken Link Building Templates

### Standard Broken Link Email

```
Subject: Broken link on your [page name]

Hi [Name],

I was reading your [page/article name] and found a broken
link—the link to [original resource topic] is returning a 404.

I have a resource that covers similar ground:
[Your URL]

It [brief description of what your resource covers].

Thought you'd want to know either way!

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Broken Link with Multiple Issues

```
Subject: A few broken links on [page name]

Hi [Name],

Going through your [page name] resource and noticed a couple
of broken links:

• [Broken link topic 1] — 404
• [Broken link topic 2] — 404

I have a resource that could work for [specific one]:
[Your URL]

It covers [brief description].

Happy to suggest alternatives for the others if helpful.

[Your name]
```

### Broken Link Follow-Up

```
Subject: Re: Broken link on [page name]

Hi [Name],

Just checking if you saw my note about the broken link on
your [page name].

The link to [topic] is still showing as broken. My resource
at [URL] could work as a replacement if useful.

Either way, hope this helps!

[Your name]
```

## Resource Page Templates

### Standard Resource Request

```
Subject: Resource for your [page name]

Hi [Name],

Found your [page name] while researching [topic]—great
collection of resources.

I have a [brief description] that might be a good addition.
It covers [specific aspect your current resources don't fully
address].

Here's the link: [URL]

Let me know if you think it fits.

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Resource Page with Specific Fit

```
Subject: Suggestion for your [topic] resources

Hi [Name],

I was looking at your [page name] and noticed you include
[type of resources they list].

I recently created a [content type] that covers [topic]
specifically. It might work well alongside your [name of
similar existing resource] since it focuses on [different
angle/audience/approach].

Here it is: [URL]

Either way, thanks for maintaining such a useful page.

[Your name]
```

## Unlinked Mention Templates

### Brand Mention Request

```
Subject: Thanks for mentioning [Brand Name]!

Hi [Name],

Just came across your article on [topic]—thanks for
mentioning [Brand Name]!

Would you be open to adding a link so readers can find us
more easily?

Here's the URL: [Your URL]

The mention is in the section about [specific location].

Thanks again for including us.

Best,
[Your name]
[Your company]
```

### Research/Data Citation Request

```
Subject: Link to our [research/study] in your article

Hi [Name],

I noticed you referenced our [research/study/data] in your
piece on [topic]. Thanks for the mention!

Would you be able to link to the original so readers can
explore the full data?

Here's the link: [URL]

Happy to answer any questions about the research.

[Your name]
```

## Link Reclamation Templates

### Lost Link Recovery

```
Subject: Link on your [article name]

Hi [Name],

I noticed our link was removed from your article on [topic]
during what looks like a recent update.

Our [resource] at [URL] still provides [relevant value] for
readers interested in [topic].

Would you be open to restoring it? Happy to discuss if
there's different content that would be a better fit now.

Thanks,
[Your name]
```

### Redirect Update Request

```
Subject: Quick fix for link on [page]

Hi [Name],

Just noticed the link to our site on your [page name] is
pointing to an old URL that redirects.

Would you be able to update it to point directly to:
[correct URL]

Quick fix that'll help readers get straight to the content.

Thanks!
[Your name]
```

## Relationship Building Templates

### Initial Connection (No Ask)

```
Subject: [Topic]—enjoyed your recent piece

Hi [Name],

I've been following your work on [topic] and wanted to say
your recent piece on [specific article] really resonated.

[One specific thing you appreciated about it—shows you
actually read it.]

I work on [your area] and would love to connect. I think
there's natural overlap in what we cover.

No ask here—just wanted to introduce myself.

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Collaboration Inquiry

```
Subject: Potential collaboration idea

Hi [Name],

I've admired your coverage of [topic] for a while, especially
[specific work].

I'm working on [project/content] and wondered if you'd be
interested in [specific collaboration idea—quote, contribution,
joint piece, etc.].

Would you be open to discussing?

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Follow-Up Sequence

### First Follow-Up (3-5 days)

```
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [Name],

Just floating this back up in case it got buried.

[One-sentence reminder of what you offered/asked]

Here's the link again: [URL]

Let me know if you have questions.

[Your name]
```

### Second Follow-Up (5-7 days after first)

```
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [Name],

Following up one more time on [topic].

Completely understand if this isn't a fit or timing is bad—
just wanted to make sure you saw it.

[URL if relevant]

Either way, thanks for your time.

[Your name]
```

### Final Follow-Up (Optional)

```
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [Name],

Last note from me on this—I know you're busy.

If [your offer] isn't relevant right now, no worries. Happy
to stay connected for the future.

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Subject Line Best Practices

### What Works

- **Specific**: Reference their content
- **Clear**: What the email is about
- **Brief**: Under 50 characters
- **Honest**: No clickbait

### Examples by Template Type

**Guest posting**:
- "Article idea: [Specific topic]"
- "Pitch for [Publication]"

**Broken links**:
- "Broken link on [page name]"
- "Quick fix for your [topic] page"

**Resource pages**:
- "Resource for your [page name]"
- "Suggestion for your [topic] resources"

**Mentions**:
- "Thanks for mentioning [Brand]!"
- "Link to our [resource] in your article"
