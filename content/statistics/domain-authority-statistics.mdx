## Important Caveats

### What DA Doesn't Tell You

1. **Not a Google metric** - DA is a third-party estimate
2. **Doesn't guarantee rankings** - Low DA pages can outrank high DA
3. **Can be manipulated** - PBN links can inflate DA
4. **Updates periodically** - Not real-time accuracy
5. **Ignores content quality** - Pure link-based metric

### Using DA Wisely

- Use as one signal among many
- Compare apples to apples (same niche)
- Focus on trends, not absolute numbers
- Always verify with traffic data
