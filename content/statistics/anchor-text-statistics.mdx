Anchor text optimization is one of the most nuanced aspects of link building. Get it wrong, and you risk penalties. Get it right, and you amplify your ranking potential. This collection presents **28 verified statistics** about anchor text distribution, ratios, and best practices.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Branded anchors dominate natural profiles** (35-50% of all anchors)
2. **Exact match anchors should be &lt;5%** of your total profile
3. **Over-optimization threshold**: &gt;50% exact match triggers penalties
4. **Natural profiles are highly diverse** - no single anchor type dominates
5. **URL anchors are underutilized** but appear in 15-25% of natural profiles

## Anchor Text Audit Framework

### Evaluating Your Anchor Profile

| Step | What to Check | Red Flags |
|------|---------------|-----------|
| 1 | Export all anchors | Missing data |
| 2 | Categorize by type | &lt;50 unique anchors |
| 3 | Calculate percentages | Any type &gt;50% |
| 4 | Check exact match | &gt;10% exact match |
| 5 | Analyze trends | Sudden changes |
| 6 | Compare to competitors | Major deviations |

### Action Thresholds

| Finding | Action Required |
|---------|----------------|
| Exact match &lt;5% | Maintain current strategy |
| Exact match 5-15% | Dilute with branded/natural |
| Exact match 15-30% | Active dilution campaign |
| Exact match &gt;30% | Disavow + aggressive dilution |
