## Benchmarking Your Campaigns

### Performance Tiers

| Metric | Below Average | Average | Above Average | Excellent |
|--------|--------------|---------|---------------|-----------|
| Open rate | &lt;35% | 35-50% | 50-60% | &gt;60% |
| Response rate | &lt;5% | 5-10% | 10-15% | &gt;15% |
| Positive response | &lt;25% | 25-40% | 40-55% | &gt;55% |
| Link conversion | &lt;1% | 1-2% | 2-4% | &gt;4% |

### Diagnostic Questions

If your metrics are below average:

1. **Low open rates**: Test subject lines, check deliverability
2. **Low response rates**: Improve personalization, add follow-ups
3. **Low positive rate**: Better targeting, stronger value proposition
4. **Low conversion**: Improve negotiation, offer more value
